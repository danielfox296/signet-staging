A jazz duo is lovely for dinner, a singer with a guitar can play the retiree's favorite song, and live band karaoke is a lot of fun if the department wants to make some noise.

Sizes

Choosing the right size band for a retirement party.

The speeches are the heart of a retirement party, so you’ll want music that leaves space for them. We can help you pick the right size for your venue.

The jazz duo, or one guitar
Upright bass and guitar together, or one jazz guitarist on their own, can play jazz standards quietly through dinner. Jazz standards work well for guests of all ages, which makes this an excellent choice for a table of colleagues. Learn more about our jazz duo.
A pianist
A pianist can play jazz standards and songs from the retiree’s favorite decades through cocktails and dinner, on the venue’s piano or on one we bring. A solo pianist fits a restaurant’s private dining space as easily as a club.
A singer with a guitar
One voice and a guitar can play the songs everyone at the office knows, and it's a warm, personal choice for a smaller party. If you’d like a fuller sound, Tejas Singh is a singer and guitarist who plays as a duo with a bass player.
Live band karaoke for the department
The band plays the 80s and 90s hits and hands the mic to anyone in the department who wants a song. Let us know the retiring colleague’s karaoke song, and the band can have it ready. Live band karaoke works best in a large private space or a hotel event space.
The party band
A trio or a quartet is the way to go when you want the send-off to end on the dance floor. Tony Medina can bring a band of up to four players and play through dinner first, and a DJ can keep the music going late through the same sound system.

Who throws it

Retirement parties for a company, a family or a department.

The company
A company retirement dinner, in a private dining space or a hotel, often falls to whoever plans the holiday party. We’re happy to help with it.
The family
A family retirement party might be a dinner at home or at a Denver club, with a toast or two, and we can build the song list with you around the music the retiree loves.
The department
If the whole office is coming to the send-off, you may want something everyone can join in on. Live band karaoke is perfect for that, and it can also be the after-party if you’re having a dinner first.

Questions

What people ask about retirement party music.

Can we hire a band for a retirement party?
Yes. You'd book the whole thing with us at Signet Artists in Denver, on one contract and with one personal contact.
How does the music work around the speeches?
The duo can play at a level your guests can talk over, stop for each speech and start up again once the glasses come down. We can also set up a microphone for whoever's speaking. Just tell us roughly when the speeches will happen, and we'll plan the music around them.
Can the company pay by purchase order?
Yes. A company's retirement dinner can be booked like any corporate event, with vendor setup, a purchase order and whatever paperwork your finance team needs. Just let us know.
How much space does a band need at a retirement party?
One guitarist needs about 6 by 4 feet, a duo about 8 by 6 and a trio about 12 by 8, and they can all run on one electrical circuit. A quartet or live band karaoke needs about 14 by 10 and two circuits, and karaoke also has two lyric screens. Setup can take about 45 minutes for one musician and an hour for a duo or a trio, and we'll work out the setup, power and volume with your venue ahead of time.
