Sizes

What size band works for an anniversary party?

In our experience, most anniversary parties are a seated dinner with a few toasts, so a duo is a great place to start. We can go bigger from there.

The jazz duo
Upright bass and guitar make a warm, polished sound, and they can play jazz standards all the way through dinner. Learn more about our jazz duo.
A singer with a guitar
One voice and a guitar are perfect if you’d like the couple’s song sung rather than played. Tejas Singh would be a lovely fit. He can start as a duo with a bass player, and the rest of his band can join him if you want dancing.
A Spanish guitarist for a long dinner
Spanish guitar is a lovely sound for a 50th anniversary dinner with the whole family. Want a fuller sound? Dirty Flamenco is our flamenco trio.
A band for a 25th anniversary with dancing
A trio or a quartet works well if the friends who danced at the wedding are coming back to do it again. Tony Medina can bring up to a four-piece band, and the same band can play dinner first. Later on, a DJ can take over through the same sound system.

Curious what each size costs in Colorado? You’ll find a general market view in our cost guide.

The party

Surprise parties, anniversary dinners & vow renewals

Surprise anniversary parties
We can have a musician start the couple’s song as they walk in, the same way we’d plan a surprise proposal song.
Anniversary dinners at home or at a club
A duo fits well into a home or a Denver club, and for many couples that’s all the music they’ll want.
Vow renewals
Some couples renew their vows at a 25th or 50th anniversary. A guitarist or a duo can play the walk in, the song the couple chose and the walk out, much like wedding ceremony music, and we can stay to play dinner afterward, too.

Questions

What to know about anniversary party music

Can we hire a band for an anniversary party?
Yes. Signet Artists books bands and smaller groups for anniversary parties across Denver and the Front Range, and we'd love to help with yours.
Can the band play the couple's first-dance song?
Yes. Tell us the title with the date, and whether it should be played or sung, and we can usually learn the song from the wedding if it isn't already on the song list.
How does a surprise anniversary party work with a musician?
The musician can set up early and play quietly, so it just looks like there's live music that night. We'll work out a cue with you ahead of time, like a nod from the host, and the couple's song can start right on it.
How loud is the music at dinner?
It's about as loud as the conversation at the table, so the toasts are easy to hear and nobody has to lean in to talk. The duo can stop for each toast, and we can have a microphone ready for whoever wants to speak. The musicians can adjust the volume to fit your venue, so a private dining space at a restaurant will need less sound than a backyard.
How much space does the band need at a dinner?
A guitarist or a singer with a guitar can fit in about 6 by 4 feet, a duo in 8 by 6, a trio in 12 by 8 and a quartet in 14 by 10. In many cases, one power source is enough, though a quartet needs two. Setup can take about 45 minutes for a solo musician and an hour for a duo or a trio. If the venue asks for a certificate of insurance, just let us know.
