Types of showers

Music for baby showers, bridal showers and couples showers.

The baby shower
A baby shower is often an afternoon at home or in a garden, with family and friends together. One guitarist can play through the meal, and we can add a song for the mother-to-be if there’s one she’d love to hear.
The bridal shower
A guitarist or a jazz duo can play standards through lunch. A singer can play the couple’s song if you’d like. We can also bring the same jazz duo back for the cocktail hour on the wedding day, and it can all go on one contract.
The couples shower
A couples shower can happen in the evening on a patio, with both partners and their friends invited, so it feels more like a small party than a lunch. A singer with a guitar or a duo is a great fit here, just like at a backyard party.

What we can bring

Shower entertainment ideas.

Showers tend to happen around a table, so we like music that fits in a corner and stays soft enough for your guests to keep talking.

A guitarist for the meal
A jazz guitarist or a Spanish guitarist can play through lunch at home or in a private dining space. Our solo guitarist plays jazz standards, and if you’d prefer Spanish guitar, we have a solo Spanish guitarist as well as the trio Dirty Flamenco.
A singer taking requests
One voice and a guitar can play the songs your guests ask for. A singer and guitarist from our roster can do this, and Tejas Singh builds songs live with a looper (a pedal that repeats what he plays), with a bass player alongside him.
A song for the guest of honor
It might be the couple’s song, a lullaby or the song her mother danced to, and we can usually learn one song for the day and play it as a surprise song.
A jazz duo for a bigger shower, or a brunch
Upright bass and guitar can fill out a bigger shower in a garden, where one guitar might get lost, and restaurants book the same duo for a Sunday brunch. Take a look at our jazz duo.

What clients say

Questions

What hosts ask about shower music.

Can you play live music at a baby shower?
Yes. You can book it through us here at Signet Artists in Denver.
How loud is it, and how much space does it need?
It’s usually about as loud as the conversation at the table, so everyone can hear each other easily. One guitarist needs about 6 by 4 feet and one electrical circuit, plus about 45 minutes to set up, and a duo needs about 8 by 6. Either one fits in the corner of a dining space or under cover in a garden. A guitarist or a duo can play for 45 to 60 minutes at a time, and we can keep music going during the breaks. If the venue asks for a certificate of insurance, just let us know.
Can the music work outdoors in a garden?
Yes. We’ll just want a covered spot out of direct sun, with power nearby. If there's no outlet nearby, a guitarist can play through a battery-powered sound system.

Send us the date, the space and roughly how many people are coming.

We can bring one guitarist or a jazz duo with sound that’s right for your space, and one personal contact will look after you for the day.
