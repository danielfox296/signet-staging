Your options

Should you book a DJ, a party band or live band karaoke?

Most Halloween parties are more about dancing than dinner, so we’d usually start with one of these three.

The DJ
A DJ is usually the first thing people ask us about, and it’s the easiest choice for a house party. Our DJs can build a mix around the decade your guests grew up in and play your must-have songs whenever you’d like to hear them.
The party band
A trio or a quartet can play the hits and get people dancing, and you won’t have to find space for a stage. Tony Medina can bring a four-piece band, and Tejas Singh leads a quartet that plays the songs everyone knows. Either band can play quietly at first if you’d like your guests to be able to talk, then turn it up when you’re ready to dance.
Live band karaoke
The band plays the 80s and 90s hits and hands the mic to anyone in costume who wants a song. Live band karaoke works best on a big main floor, in a finished basement or in a garage with the cars out.

Extras

Halloween party entertainment ideas for adults.

A costume contest
We can have a microphone ready so whoever’s judging can run the contest over the sound system, and the band or the DJ can play a song for the winner.
A grown-up Halloween dinner before the party
If you’d like a seated dinner in costume first, an upright bass and a guitar can play during the meal, and the band or the DJ can take over when the bigger crowd arrives.

Questions

What hosts ask us about Halloween parties

Planning a party at your house? Learn more about parking and power on our Denver private parties page, and check out our technical page for stage sizes.

Do you have a DJ for a Halloween party?
Yes. You can book a DJ through Signet Artists in Denver, either on their own or to take over after a band, all on one contract.
Will the band or the DJ play in costume?
Just ask when you book. It’s up to the musicians what they wear, and if you’d like a song list full of Halloween songs, we’re happy to put one together with you.
How loud is it, and how late can it run?
It’s a party, so the band or the DJ will turn it up once people are dancing. Sets usually run 45 to 60 minutes with music through the breaks, and it can all keep going as late as you and your neighbors are happy with. At a house, it helps to keep the loudest part indoors with the doors shut. If your street or HOA has quiet hours, let us know when you book and we'll plan the night around them.
How much space does a Halloween party band need?
A DJ may need about 6 by 4 feet, one dedicated electrical circuit and 45 minutes to set up. A trio may need about 12 by 8 feet and one circuit, and a quartet or the four-piece karaoke band about 14 by 10 feet and two circuits. It’s best to keep the fog machine and the string lights on a different circuit from the music. Parking can get tight when everyone on the block has guests, so let us know about the driveway or the alley and which door we should use.
