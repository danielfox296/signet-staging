The party

Engagement dinners, cocktail parties & surprise parties

The dinner with the two families
If you’re bringing both families together for dinner at home, at a Denver club or in a private dining space, one or two musicians are usually all you’ll need.
The cocktail party on a patio
In the summer, you might invite friends from both sides and make it more about the party than the meal. A singer with a guitar or a duo works well here, just as they do at a backyard party.
The party right after the proposal
Surprise the couple with everyone they love and their song already playing. Just tell us what you have in mind, and we can play the proposal, the party or both. For the wedding weekend’s welcome party, take a look at our weddings page.

Engagement party entertainment ideas.

Since most engagement parties are a dinner or a cocktail party, we’d usually suggest a duo first. From there, it depends on how much dancing you have in mind.

A jazz duo the two families can talk over
Upright bass and guitar playing jazz standards will bring some sophistication to the dinner and leave plenty of space for conversation. Want to hear them? Take a look at our jazz duo.
The couple’s song, sung live
Tejas Singh can sing your first-dance song in front of both families, and he can usually learn it if he doesn’t know it already. He’s happy to take requests for the rest of the party, too.
A Spanish guitarist for dinner
Spanish guitar gives a dinner a romantic sound, and many couples go on to choose it for their wedding ceremony, too. Looking for something bigger? Dirty Flamenco plays flamenco as a trio.
A band for a party your friends throw
When your friends throw the party, you may want a dance floor. Tony Medina can play as a quartet, and a DJ can keep the party going after they finish.

Want to hear your wedding party sing months before the wedding? With live band karaoke, they can take turns at the microphone while the band plays the hits.

Questions

What couples ask about engagement party music

Can we hire a band for an engagement party?
Yes. We can bring anything from one musician to a quartet, depending on how big your party is and whether you’d like dancing. Signet Artists is based in Denver, and you’ll have one personal contact the whole way through.
Can the same musicians play the engagement party and the wedding?
Yes, on one contract. The duo from the engagement party can play the cocktail hour, and the singer who learned the couple's song already has it for the first dance. Tell us both dates when you write.
How loud is it?
It’s quiet enough for both families to hear each other across the table, and the musicians can stop for each toast. We can have an announcement microphone ready for the toasts. A trio can play dinner at that same volume and turn up when it’s time to dance.
Can the band play on a patio?
Yes, as long as the musicians are under cover and there’s an outlet nearby. You can fit a singer with a guitar in about 6 by 4 feet with just one outlet, a duo in about 8 by 6 and a trio in about 12 by 8. Once the sun goes down, it’s best to move the louder music indoors or switch to the DJ. If you need a certificate of insurance, just let us know.
