Where to start

Which act fits which kind of event?

Most people book by the kind of event first and the sound second, so here’s the roster sorted that way.

A cocktail hour, a dinner, or background music
For a cocktail hour or a dinner, book a solo guitarist, a singer and guitarist, a Spanish guitarist, a jazz duo, an acoustic duo or a pianist. They all play at a volume your guests can talk over, and Last Consulate (one musician on upright bass and guitar over downtempo electronics) is a great choice when you want something a little more modern. In December, the Christmas jazz trio plays the season’s songs.
A party with a dance floor
A trio or a quartet is the size to book when you want people dancing. Tejas Singh and Tony Medina can both start small and grow to a four piece over the evening. The Americana band, the yacht rock band, the soul and R&B band, the millennial party rock band and the funk band each get people dancing to a different sound, and with live band karaoke, your guests take the mic.
A DJ, on its own or after the band
The DJ is open format (a mix of genres rather than one style), and can play the whole night or take over after any band on the roster through the same sound system.
A show rather than background music
Some acts are made for your guests to stop and watch. Book Dirty Flamenco, Changes Jazz Trio with a saxophone added, or René Moffatt playing his own songs. Your Bird Can Sing gets a whole crowd singing along, and Firstwater brings a sound bath to an offsite or a wellness day.
A wedding
Many couples book an acoustic or Spanish guitarist for the ceremony, a duo for the cocktail hour and the DJ for the dance floor. A wedding jazz band can cover the whole day in one style. Find out more on our weddings page.
During the day
A jazz duo or a guitarist works well for senior living, a memorial or a Sunday brunch, the Beatles sing-along is a great choice for a community afternoon, and a sound bath can be booked during the day too. Find out more on our daytime page.

What's the difference between a Featured Artist and a House Ensemble?
A Featured Artist, like Tejas Singh or Dirty Flamenco, is booked by name, so you know exactly who will be playing. A House Ensemble, like a jazz duo or a yacht rock band, is played by professional musicians we match to your date and your venue. To book either one, just send us the date.
Where do the acts play?
Our acts play in Denver, Boulder, Colorado Springs and Fort Collins, plus mountain towns like Vail, Beaver Creek, Aspen, Breckenridge, Telluride, Steamboat Springs, Winter Park and Crested Butte. If your venue is outside the Denver area, just let us know where it is and we'll work out the travel with you.
