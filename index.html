Why book through Signet

We'll put together the right music for your event.

When you book a band on your own, there's a lot to work out with the musicians, and then you have to get them all into a contract. We'll work through them with you, and the same person will be your point of contact from your first email to the end of your event.

How many musicians
A duo works beautifully at a dinner, a trio can get people dancing, and a full band has the energy for a big ballroom. We'll help you pick the right size based on your guest count, your budget and where the music fits in your event.
Sound for the space
We can bring the sound up to reach the back of a ballroom or keep it low enough that guests can talk over dinner, and we'll work within any noise limit your venue sets.
The kind of music
You might want jazz during dinner, a soul band for dancing, a string quartet for a ceremony or a DJ to finish the party. Tell us about the crowd and we’ll recommend the act that suits it.
The songs
Let us know your must-plays, the songs you'd rather skip and your first dance, and we can keep the lyrics clean for a work crowd. We'll go over the song list with you before your event.
What they wear
The musicians dress to your dress code, from black tie to business casual to all black, so they look like they belong at your event.
Photos and video
Part of a great event is being able to tell its story once it's over. We can add a photographer and a videographer who work alongside the band, so you'll have photos and a short film to keep. Pick Event Photos, a Highlight Film, both together as Photos and Film, or a Culture Film. Learn more about our photo and video packages.

All of it goes into one contract. If your venue asks for an insurance certificate, just let us know and we can send it over. Curious about the steps? Take a look at how a booking works.
