Chantil Dukart

  • HonoreeNative Launchpad Associate ArtistWestern Arts Alliance, 2023

Standards, covers and originals, from solo piano to a five-piece band.

There’s something about a singer at the piano that makes an event feel special. Chantil Dukart is a Denver jazz pianist and singer with roots in the Tsimshian and Sugpiaq peoples of Alaska, and her song list runs from Nat King Cole and Ella Fitzgerald to jazz versions of Stevie Wonder, Elvis and Frank Ocean. You can book her solo at the piano, as a duo, with her jazz trio or with a full band.

Trusted and booked by

Chantil grew up in Alaska and studied music at the University of Miami’s Frost School of Music. She’s played Miami hotels, an Australian cruise and venues in Anchorage, and now she makes her home in Denver. She also tours with the Julia Keefe Indigenous Big Band and with Pamyua, an Alaska Native group.

She’s shared the stage with Esperanza Spalding and Jill Scott, and she’s played the Kennedy Center and the Smithsonian Folklife Festival. Her grandmother’s Nat King Cole CDs back in Anchorage started her love of his music, and now she plays a tribute to him at Dazzle every year. She’s also spent years as an on-air DJ at Jazz 93.5 in Colorado Springs.

Chantil writes her own songs, too, mixing jazz with pop and soul. Her records include Lady and the Champ and Chantil and the Dukes of Art, which she made with her trio. She’s happy to work a few of her songs in with the standards, or to stick with songs everybody knows.

Occasions

Perfect booking for

Weddings
Book her solo at the piano for your ceremony and cocktail hour, then add bass and drums for dinner. Her song list has first-dance favorites like At Last and Can’t Help Falling in Love, and she loves playing classy jazz versions of a couple’s favorite songs. She’ll even turn a hip-hop song into classical piano, and if you’d like, she can write a song for your partner, too.
Galas and awards dinners
Her trio brings a lot of class to a gala or an awards dinner, and they can play anything from quiet standards to something with a little more swing.
Holiday parties
At the 2025 KUVO Jazz Holiday Celebration, her trio played holiday favorites alongside jazz originals, and that’s a lovely mix for a company holiday party.
Dinner parties and celebrations
She’s a wonderful fit for a birthday, an anniversary or a dinner party, at home or at your favorite venue.

Have toasts or a program planned? We can work the music around them with your run of show, and our cost guide gives you a general idea of what live music costs in Colorado.

Chantil Dukart questions

Still have a question? Our full question list may have the answer.

Can she play instrumental jazz?
Yes. She sings and plays piano, and she’s just as happy to play instrumental standards for part of your event, or for all of it.
Can she keep the volume down during dinner?
Yes. On her own or as a duo, she plays at a level where your guests can talk easily across the table.
Does she play outside Denver?
Yes. She plays all over Colorado, from Boulder and Golden to Nederland and Colorado Springs, and she tours around the country, too. Tell us where your event is and we’ll let you know what’s possible.
Does she sing in the Tsimshian language?
Yes, on some songs. Louie the White Raven, for one, mixes English with Smalgyax, the Tsimshian language. If you’d like something like that at your event, just let us know.
