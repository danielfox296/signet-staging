Booking

How to hire a live band for your event.

You've got a date on the calendar, and now you're wondering what happens after you reach out. Here's each step, who does it and how long it usually takes.

Booking a band, step by step

These timings assume your event is about six months away. If your event is only three weeks away, we'll go through the same steps in the same order, just faster. Planning a wedding? Here's our take on how far ahead to book wedding musicians in Colorado.

StepWhenWhat happens
1. You get in touch24 to 48 hrsWe reply with availability and an itemized quote
2. You hold the dateThe day you say yesWe send the contract, the certificate of insurance, the W-9 and the invoice in one email
3. We introduce your point of contactBefore the eventYou'll get the name and cell number of your personal point of contact for the event
4. You send the run of showAny timeWe add the band's part and send it back
5. We go over the cuesThe week beforeWe make sure one person is in charge of each moment in the program
6. The venue confirmsThe week beforeThey confirm the power, the stage, the load-in route and any decibel cap
7. Load-in and sound checkThe day of the eventWe set up, check the sound and hand the space back to you, ready to go
8. We play your eventThe day of the eventYou can reach your contact from load-in to load-out
9. The invoiceAfter the eventWe send one page, with every charge on its own line

Wondering about the deposit, when the balance is due or how to pay? Take a look at our FAQ. Want load-in and sound check times by band size, along with the six things to confirm with your venue? Check out our guide to what we need from your venue.

Three things we need from you

Six of those nine steps need nothing from you at all. The other three do, and if those run late, they can hold up your booking.

The date and the venue, to get a number
If you haven't chosen a venue yet, just give us the city. We can price your event now and update it once you've signed with a venue.
The venue name, for the certificate of insurance
The certificate names your venue as additional insured, so we can't issue it until you've chosen one.
The run of show, whenever you have one
Send us the draft, even if it's nowhere near final. We're happy to add the band's part to whatever you have, even if you haven't booked us yet.

If something changes

Plans change all the time, whether it's the guest count, the program or even the venue. Just let us know and we'll update your quote. Need more time, a smaller band or a different date? We'll work it out with you.

If a musician can't make your date, we'll tell you who's stepping in and why as soon as we know.
