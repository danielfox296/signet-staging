Golden, Colorado

Live music for a wedding cocktail hour in Golden.

Golden is just far enough from Denver that a wedding there feels like a getaway, with Lookout Mountain on one side of town and Clear Creek running through the middle. An acoustic duo is a lovely way to fill the hour between the ceremony and dinner, and it’s easy to talk over.

Planning the rest of the day? Learn more about wedding music, and for ideas on what to play, check out our cocktail hour page.

The venues

Golden wedding venues for a cocktail hour

A duo only needs a small level spot with power nearby, so it fits in a garden, a courtyard or beside a pool.

Boettcher Mansion
The mansion dates to 1917 and sits up on Lookout Mountain, and its garden is a lovely spot for cocktails. We’ll plan an indoor spot for the duo, too, in case the weather turns.
The Golden Hotel
Right downtown on Clear Creek, the hotel’s Creekside Courtyard is a lovely spot for an outdoor ceremony and the cocktail hour that follows.
Table Mountain Inn
On Washington Avenue in the middle of downtown Golden, with event space inside and a pool outside.

Golden cocktail hour questions

Getting married in Denver instead? Learn more about cocktail hour music in Denver.

Can the duo play outdoors in Golden?
Yes. It needs a level spot with cover and power within reach. Evenings at the foot of the mountains can cool off fast, so we’ll plan where the music goes if everyone moves inside.
Can the same musicians play the ceremony?
Yes. A duo or a singer with a guitar can play the processional and then move to the cocktail hour.
Is a duo enough for a cocktail hour in Golden?
For most cocktail hours, yes. If your guests will be spread across a big garden, a third musician helps the music reach everyone.
