Wedding day music

How a wedding jazz band grows through the day

The ceremony
A solo jazz guitarist or a duo can play ballads while your guests find their seats, the processional you choose, and something upbeat for the walk back up the aisle.
The cocktail hour
Upright bass and guitar make a lovely duo for cocktail hour, at a volume that’s easy to talk over. Jazz standards are a great choice here, since they’re the songs a lot of people already know and love.
Dinner
For dinner, we add drums to make a trio, and the drummer plays with brushes to keep the volume soft. The band can stop for each toast, so your speaker doesn’t have to compete with the music. Changes Jazz Trio is another great option for dinner.
The first dance and the dance floor
For the dancing, we add a horn or a singer to make a quartet, and the band can play the swing and soul side of the standards. Later on, the DJ can take over on the same sound system.

Wedding jazz bands

Which jazz band is right for your wedding?

The jazz duo and trio
We’ll start with upright bass and guitar, add drums if you’d like them, and let you know which musicians are playing when you book. This is the one to book if you care more about the sound than about a particular name.
Changes Jazz Trio
This trio plays jazz standards with a modern edge on guitar, fretless bass (a bass with a smooth, singing tone) and drums. We can add a saxophone to make a quartet for the dance floor. And with Changes Jazz Trio, you’ll know exactly who’s playing before you decide.
Chantil Dukart
If you’d love a singer at the piano, Chantil is a Denver jazz pianist and singer who can play on her own for your ceremony and bring her trio for dinner. She can even write a song for your partner.
Jazz until dinner, then something else
If you’d like jazz standards through dinner and pop and rock for the dancing, we can pair our jazz musicians with a cover band like Tejas Singh or with the DJ.

Questions

What couples ask about wedding jazz bands.

Which size of jazz band does a wedding reception need?
It depends on what’s happening at that point in the reception. The size of your venue matters, too, and for a ballroom of two hundred, we’d put a singer out front. Tell us the guest count and the venue and we'll recommend the size. Signet Artists books wedding jazz bands across Denver and the Colorado mountains.
How loud is a jazz band at a wedding?
At the ceremony and through cocktail hour, it’s gentle enough for your guests to greet each other over it. At dinner, the band stays at about the level of the conversation at your tables. For the dancing, the quartet can play like a real dance band, up to whatever your venue allows.
Will a jazz band play our first dance?
Yes. If the song is a standard, it's probably already on our song list. If it isn't, send us the title with your date and we can usually learn it.
Is a jazz band the right choice if we want dancing at our wedding?
It can be, if your guests love dancing to swing and soul standards.
