Flick of the Whisk: wedding cakes and cupcakes in Denver.

A wedding cake has to look beautiful in the photos and taste great afterward. Flick of the Whisk is a great choice if you care about both.

Bryonna founded Flick of the Whisk in Denver in 2019. She’s self-taught, and her cakes are minimalist and modern, with a subtle edge.

Flick of the Whisk at a glance

What she makes
She makes wedding and celebration cakes, cupcakes and mini cupcakes, and she’s added sheet cakes, brownie bites and chocolate chip cookies to the menu.
Where she works
She’s based in Denver, and she makes cakes for weddings across Colorado.
Dietary needs
Vegan and gluten-free versions are available for most of her flavors.
Tasting
You can order a tasting box to try her flavors before you decide.

Wedding cakes in Denver

Bryonna’s style is curated minimalism: clean lines, careful details and the colors you choose, with flowers or a custom topper if you’d like one.

Her flavor menu runs from classics like vanilla and marble to Strawberry Dreams, Zesty Lemon and Just Peachy, a cinnamon cake with peach filling.

Her cakes run from a small 4-inch cake for an elopement to a three-tier statement cake, and with a tiered cake you can save the top tier to freeze.

Wedding cakes by Flick of the Whisk

All of the cakes on this page are by Bryonna, and the photographs come from her gallery.

Cupcakes and dessert tables

If you’d like cupcakes instead of a big cake, or alongside one, Bryonna makes standard and mini cupcakes in your color palette, with custom toppers if you like. Her 4-ounce jar cupcakes come individually packaged with custom labels, so they double as favors.

You can see more of her cakes in her gallery, and her menu page has every flavor. To check your date, use the order form on that page.
