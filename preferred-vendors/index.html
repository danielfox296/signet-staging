Planning and coordination

Need someone to run the day? We’ll give you the name of a planner we’ve worked with.

Officiants

Michelle Cohn Levy

Vail

Michelle leads Jewish and secular weddings in Vail and across the Colorado mountains, and she can lead a Jewish ceremony on her own or with a rabbi. She’s also a singer, so she can sing parts of your ceremony herself.

Read more about Michelle Cohn Levy

Photography

MXBloom Films

Denver and Seattle

Neil and Fiona are a husband-and-wife team who take bright, lovely wedding photographs across Colorado and Washington. They’re a great choice if you’d like a natural, story-driven look.

Read more about MXBloom Films

Videography

MXBloom Films

Denver and Seattle

The same team makes emotional, cinematic wedding films, so you can have your photographs and your film from the same two people.

Hair and makeup

Makeup by Sameera

Denver

Sameera Ahmed is a Denver makeup and hair artist whose bridal looks range from soft and natural to bold. She’s a great choice if you’d like a look that represents your own style.

Read more about Makeup by Sameera

Grace Martin

Denver

Grace Martin is a Denver hair and makeup artist who also works in film, television and national campaigns. Her bridal work has appeared in Rocky Mountain Bride, and she works with all hair textures and skin tones.

Read more about Grace Martin

Divine Beauty Artists

Denver

Christy Kaliana and Kemper run a team of licensed bridal hair and makeup artists based around Denver, who work in Boulder, Breckenridge, Vail and across Colorado. They’re a great fit when you need hair and makeup for a whole wedding party.

Read more about Divine Beauty Artists

Denver

Prairie Hill Flower Co.

Parker
