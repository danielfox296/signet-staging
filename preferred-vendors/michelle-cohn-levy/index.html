Michelle Cohn Levy: a wedding officiant in Vail.

Your ceremony is the part of the day your families came for, and it helps a lot to have someone leading it who knows the traditions and can make everyone feel welcome. Michelle Cohn Levy leads Jewish and secular weddings in Vail and across the Colorado mountains. For a Jewish ceremony, she can lead on her own or alongside a rabbi.

She’s also a singer, so she can sing parts of your ceremony herself, and she leads B’nai Mitzvah ceremonies and Jewish services, too.

Michelle Cohn Levy at a glance

What she offers
She officiates Jewish and secular weddings, and Bar and Bat Mitzvahs. For a Jewish ceremony, she can lead on her own or with a rabbi. She also tutors B’nai Mitzvah students, in person or online.
Where she works
She lives in Vail, and she’s led weddings in Vail, Beaver Creek, Bachelor Gulch, Aspen, Camp Hale, Piney Lake and Denver. She’s traveled for weddings in Chicago, Orlando and Maryland, too.
Jewish services
She’s led hundreds of services in Reform, Conservative, Reconstructionist and non-denominational settings, for congregations of thousands and for small groups in a living room. She leads Friday evening Shabbat services for B’nai Vail.
Music for the ceremony
She can sing during your ceremony, and she’s arranged all of the music for a wedding with cello, violin, piano and vocals.

Jewish and secular wedding ceremonies in Vail

Michelle leads the whole ceremony, from signing the ketubah to breaking the glass. Her couples often mention that she made the traditions easy to follow for guests who didn’t know them, so everyone felt included.

She officiates secular weddings, too, so she can lead your ceremony whether or not you’d like the religious traditions in it.

She plans the ceremony with you, gets to know you as a couple and builds it around what you’d like. Couples on her site also write about how flexible she is on the day: one moved their ceremony to a new spot on Beaver Creek Mountain at the last minute, and another saw rain coming, so she picked up the pace and got everyone under the tent in time.

Weddings with Michelle Cohn Levy

Bar and Bat Mitzvah officiant in Vail

If your family is planning a Bar or Bat Mitzvah in the mountains, Michelle can officiate on her own or with a rabbi. She tutors students in person or online, too, so your child can prepare with the same person who’ll lead the service.

You can see more of her ceremonies on her weddings page and her B’nai Mitzvah page. To check your date, reach out to Michelle through her website.
