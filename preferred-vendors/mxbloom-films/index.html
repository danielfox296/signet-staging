MXBloom Films: Denver wedding photography and video.

A lot of couples hire a photographer and a videographer separately, and then hope the two of them work well together on the day. With MXBloom Films, it’s one team.

Neil and Fiona are a husband-and-wife team based in Denver and Seattle, and they personally handle both the photography and the filmmaking. Their photographs are bright and vibrant, and their wedding films are emotional and cinematic.

MXBloom Films at a glance

What they offer
They offer wedding photography and cinematography, and they have combination packages if you want both.
Where they work
They work throughout Colorado and Washington, and at destination weddings too.
Experience
They have nearly 10 years of experience and have photographed and filmed more than 300 weddings.
Awards
They’ve received WeddingWire Couples’ Choice and The Knot Best of Weddings awards from 2020 to 2025.

One team for your photographs and your film

When one team takes care of the photographs and the film, you have fewer people to coordinate, and they’re already working from the same plan.

Neil and Fiona describe their style as a mix of documentary and editorial. They work in the background for most of the day and step in with some gentle direction when it’s needed, which is a relief if you feel awkward in front of a camera. They also take just one event per day, so your wedding has their full attention.

A wedding film carries your vows and your first dance, so the sound matters as much as the picture. Neil and Fiona bring professional audio recording along with their cameras.

This is the team to book when you want your photographs and your film from the same two people, with a natural, story-driven look.
