We book live music for weddings, corporate events and private parties across Colorado, so we know what a planner wants to see before they hire a band. We shoot promo photos and live video for musicians with those questions in mind.

What do event planners look for in a band?

When a planner is choosing between a few acts, they're usually asking three questions.

Can I see them play to a real crowd?
A clear live clip with good sound tells a planner more than any bio can.
Will they look right at my event?
Photos that show how you dress and carry yourself on stage help them picture you at their event.
Do they seem professional?
Current photos that match across your website, your socials and your booking pages tell them you're organized and easy to work with.

Promo shoots and live video for bands

Promo shoot
For acts that need current photos for a website, socials, press and booking pages. You'll get edited photos, sized for the web and social media, shot at a real venue or in a performance setting.
Live video
For acts that need a clip a planner can watch and trust. You'll get edited performance clips with clean sound, cut for booking pages and social media.

Want both? We can plan the photos and the video together in one session.

How a shoot works

Tell us about your act
Send links to the photos, video and website you have now, and let us know what you need.
Get a quote and plan the shoot
We'll send you a quote, and then we'll plan the look, the location and the songs that show you at your best.
