Looking for more event gigs in Colorado?

Signet books live music for weddings, private parties and corporate events in Denver, Boulder, Colorado Springs, Fort Collins, Vail, Beaver Creek, Aspen, Breckenridge, Telluride, Steamboat Springs, Winter Park and Crested Butte. We look after the client side of every booking, from the first inquiry through the quote, the contract, the deposit and the details on the day, so that you can show up and play.

We represent solo acts, duos, trios and quartets, and we put a larger band together when a client asks for one. We book jazz, flamenco and Spanish guitar, funk, soul and R&B, blues, classic rock, yacht rock, Americana and bluegrass, country, Latin, pop and rock covers, string players, pianists, singer-songwriters and DJs, and if you play something else, send it anyway.

Representation is non-exclusive, so you keep your own gigs and your own clients.

What we need to consider an act.

Live video
Send us a clip of you playing to a crowd. A phone recording from the back of the venue is fine, and it’s the first thing we open. No good live video yet? We can help with that too, so take a look at artist development.
A song list
We’re looking for enough songs for a three-hour event, and most of them should be songs the crowd already knows. Paste it in the form or link it.
Your own PA
You’ll need a sound system that fits the venues you play, from a dinner party to a courtyard, and someone who can run it.
Liability insurance (or you’re willing to get it)
Many venues ask for a certificate of insurance before the event. Insurance Canopy writes performer policies by the year, and MusicPro covers the gear.
How you dress
Your clothes should suit a wedding or a corporate dinner. We ask for business casual at least, and we’ll let you know when a client wants something dressier.
The set format
We usually ask for sets of 45 to 60 minutes, with 15 to 20 minutes between them and music playing through the break.
A quick answer on a date
When we write to ask about a date, a reply the same day keeps the booking moving.

Want to play events with us?

To get started, we just need links to live video, your song list, the sizes you offer and your typical rate for a three-hour event. We read every submission, and when there’s a fit we’ll write back and set up a call.
