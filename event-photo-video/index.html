Photos and video to tell the story of your event.

Part of what makes a great event is being able to tell its story after it's over. We can add a photographer and a videographer to your booking, so you'll have photos and a short film of something that brought everyone together.

How companies use their event photos and video

A highlight video helps the event live on as part of your company's history and culture.

Recruiting
Candidates get a real look at your team, and at what it's like to be part of it.
Showing potential clients your culture
A few minutes of your team celebrating together gives a client a feel for the people they'd be working with.
On stage at future events
This year's highlight video can open next year's party, a kickoff or an awards program.

Booking photos and video with your music

One point of contact
The same person looks after your music and your photos and video.
Planned around the music
Your photographer works from the same plan as the band, so they know where the music is set up and which moments matter most to you.
One contract
Photos and video go on the same contract as your music, so there's no second vendor to find, vet and manage.
Delivery
You'll usually have your photos and video within a week of the event (depending on the project).

Event photo and video questions

How much do event photos and video cost?
We quote photos and video together with your music, one date at a time, so you won't find prices for them on the site. Send us the date and the package you have in mind, and we'll get back to you with the total.
Can I add photos or video after I've booked the music?
Yes. Just let us know, and we'll check the date and add it to your booking.
Can you photograph or film our wedding?
For wedding-day photography or film, take a look at the photographers and videographers on our Preferred Vendor List.
