Hispanic Heritage Month ideas for the office: a mariachi band

Hispanic Heritage Month runs from September 15 to October 15. We’d put a mariachi band and a taco truck in the middle of a workday. Here is when it fits and what to pair it with.

Signet Artists · November 14, 2026 · 2 min read

Someone at the company has been asked to plan something for Hispanic Heritage Month. There’s a date range, a budget and a blank page.

We’d fill it with a mariachi band during the workday and Mexican food to go with it.

When is Hispanic Heritage Month? #

September 15 to October 15, every year. It began as a week in 1968 and became a month in 1988. September 15 is the independence day of five Central American countries, and Mexico’s follows on September 16. In 2027, September 16 is a Thursday.

Why a mariachi band? #

Our favorite kind of event takes people somewhere else. Sounds people don’t hear every day make an event more memorable, especially alongside the food, the decor and a fully formed theme.

A mariachi needs no build-up. The band walks in dressed, the trumpets start, and by the end of the first song an ordinary Thursday is an occasion.

Our mariachi band is usually six or seven musicians in charro suits, with violins, trumpet, vihuela, guitar and guitarrón, and several of them sing. They play rancheras, boleros, huapangos and cumbias.

When in the day does it fit? #

The workday is best, and any part of it.

The band plays outdoors often, so a courtyard or the lot beside the truck works as well as a lobby or a cafeteria.

What should you pair it with? #

Mexican food, and the real thing. A taco truck parked outside is the easy answer.

Is mariachi a Colorado tradition? #

It is now. More Colorado schools teach mariachi alongside jazz band and orchestra, and Metropolitan State University of Denver offers the state’s first degree in it.

What should you tell us? #

The date, the headcount, the part of the day, and whether it’s indoors or out. Our corporate page covers how we work with a company, and How to make a corporate event sound like Colorado covers the rest of the state’s music.
