Chantil Dukart joins Signet Artists

Denver jazz pianist and singer Chantil Dukart, who has played the Kennedy Center and tours with the Julia Keefe Indigenous Big Band, is now one of our Featured Artists.

Signet Artists · November 13, 2026 · 2 min read

We have some lovely news if you’ve been hoping for a singer at the piano. Chantil Dukart is now one of our Featured Artists, and we’re so happy to have her on our Artist Roster!

Chantil is a Denver jazz pianist and singer with roots in the Tsimshian and Sugpiaq peoples of Alaska. She studied music at the University of Miami’s Frost School of Music, and she’s played everywhere from Miami hotels to Dazzle in Denver.

Chantil and her trio playing the standard Imagination live at Rocky Mountain Recorders in Denver.

Where Chantil Dukart has played #

Chantil has shared the stage with Esperanza Spalding and Jill Scott, and she’s performed at the Kennedy Center and the Smithsonian Folklife Festival. She tours with the Julia Keefe Indigenous Big Band and Pamyua, too, and SAY Magazine called her “a powerhouse on and off stage.”

Around Colorado, she’s played the Colorado Chautauqua in Boulder and the Ned Jazz & Wine Festival, and her trio played the 2025 KUVO Jazz Holiday Celebration.

Booking Chantil Dukart for your event #

On her own at the piano, Chantil is a lovely choice for a wedding ceremony, a cocktail hour or a dinner party. She can add upright bass for a duo, bring her jazz trio, Chantil and the Dukes of Art, or lead a five-piece band with guitar and tenor saxophone.

For weddings, she loves playing classy jazz versions of a couple’s favorite songs, and she can even write a song for your partner.

What Chantil Dukart plays #

Her song list runs from Nat King Cole and Ella Fitzgerald to jazz versions of Stevie Wonder, Elvis and Frank Ocean, and she plays her own songs, too. Want to hear more? Check out Chantil’s video clips and song list.

Just send us your date and venue, and we’ll let you know if she’s available.
