Roma Ransom joins Signet Artists

Roma Ransom, the violin and guitar duo of Grace Easley and Gordon Lewis, is now one of our Featured Artists.

Signet Artists · November 11, 2026 · 2 min read

We have some exciting news if you’ve been looking for a violin and guitar duo in Colorado. Roma Ransom is now one of our Featured Artists, and we’re thrilled to have them on our Artist Roster!

Grace Easley and Gordon Lewis met busking on a street in Springfield, Missouri, and they’ve played together ever since. They’re married now, they live in Colorado Springs, and they play all over the Front Range and the mountains.

Roma Ransom on the East Music Stage at the Parker Days Festival in Parker, Colorado.

What Roma Ransom plays #

They named the duo for the music of Romania, and their song list goes from Romanian and Eastern European folk to old-time ballads like Shady Grove and St. James Infirmary, swing tunes like Caravan and their own songs.

Both of them trained classically from an early age, Grace on violin and viola and Gordon on guitar. Grace sings, too, so they can play a whole set of instrumental tunes or mix in the ballads and blues.

Roma Ransom’s stages and credits #

For about a decade they’ve played more than 200 shows a year across North America, and they’ve shared festival bills with Leftover Salmon and Larry and His Flask. They’ve played Dazzle in Denver and a VIP event at the Pikes Peak Center for an Itzhak Perlman performance, and Colorado Public Radio featured them on Air Check.

Their music has also been in films and ads for Patagonia, Yeti, Huckberry and Bud Light.

Booking Roma Ransom for your event #

Violin and guitar make a lovely wedding ceremony, and Roma Ransom is a great fit for a cocktail hour, a dinner party or a banquet when you want music with some character. They bring their own sound system, so outdoor events are easy, too. Want to hear more? Check out Roma Ransom’s video clips.
