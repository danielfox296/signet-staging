Meghan Clarisse joins Signet Artists

Denver singer-songwriter Meghan Clarisse is now one of our Featured Artists. Here’s a little about her, what she plays and how you can book her.

Signet Artists · November 7, 2026 · 2 min read

If you’ve been looking for a country singer for a wedding or a party in Denver, we have some great news. Meghan Clarisse is now one of our Featured Artists, and we’re so happy to have her!

Meghan is a Denver singer-songwriter who plays country and Americana, and she calls it “music with country roots and cowgirl boots.” Colorado viewers may have seen her on Great Day Colorado or Colorado & Company.

Meghan and her band playing Fire and Fly, one of her own songs.

Meghan Clarisse’s awards #

Her album Shadows of a Ghost Town won the 2026 Josie Music Award for Folk/Americana Album of the Year. Also in 2026, the Colorado Country Music Hall of Fame gave her its Golden Star Award, one year after her duo won the Hall of Fame’s Duo of the Year award.

Off stage, Meghan volunteers with Musicians On Call and plays for patients at Children’s Hospital and the VA in Denver.

Booking Meghan Clarisse for your event #

You have three sizes to choose from. On her own, Meghan sings with an acoustic guitar or sometimes keys, which is lovely for welcome drinks, a patio or a dinner at home.

Her duo adds pedal steel or electric lead guitar. Pedal steel is the sliding guitar on classic country records, so if you want that sound, it’s a great one to ask for.

Her five-piece band is the one to book when you want people dancing at a wedding reception or a big party.

What Meghan Clarisse plays #

Alongside her own songs, she plays covers like Jolene, Wagon Wheel, Dreams, Cover Me Up, Mama Tried and Landslide, and we’ll put the setlist together with you. Want to see more? Check out Meghan’s photos and song list.

Want to book Meghan for your event?

Just send us your date and venue, and we’ll let you know if she’s available.
