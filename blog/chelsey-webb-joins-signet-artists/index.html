Chelsey Webb joins Signet Artists

Country singer Chelsey Webb, the Colorado Country Music Hall of Fame’s 2025 Female Entertainer of the Year, is now one of our Featured Artists.

Signet Artists · November 10, 2026 · 2 min read

We have some exciting news if you’ve been hoping to book a country band in Denver. Chelsey Webb is now one of our Featured Artists, and we’re thrilled to have her on our Artist Roster!

Chelsey grew up in Alabama, recorded in Nashville and now makes her home in Denver. She has a big, smoky voice, a lot of Southern gospel in her background and, in her own words, a little bit of outlaw.

Chelsey and her band playing a dance medley of Shut Up and Dance, I Wanna Dance with Somebody and Girls Just Want to Have Fun, into Here for the Party.

Chelsey Webb’s award and stages #

In 2025 the Colorado Country Music Hall of Fame named Chelsey its Female Entertainer of the Year, and The Denver Gazette was there for it.

You may have seen her at Cheyenne Frontier Days, the Greeley Stampede or the National Western Stock Show. She’s also shared the stage with country artists like Tracy Byrd, Jackson Dean and Corey Kent.

Booking Chelsey Webb for your event #

You can book Chelsey as a duo with her husband on acoustic guitar, which is a warm fit for a cocktail hour, a dinner or a party at home.

When you want people dancing, bring in her full band. It has fiddle, acoustic and electric guitar and drums, and the six-piece adds pedal steel for that classic country sound.

What Chelsey Webb plays #

Her song list goes from Sweet Home Alabama, Neon Moon and Here for the Party to Party in the U.S.A. and Espresso, and she plays her own songs too. Want to see more? Check out Chelsey’s video clips and song list.

Want to book Chelsey for your event?

Just send us your date and venue, and we’ll let you know if she’s available.
