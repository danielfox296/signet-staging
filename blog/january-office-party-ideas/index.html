January office party ideas

Thinking about holding your company party after the holidays? Here’s why a January date works, seven ways to plan it and some themes that work without holiday decor.

Signet Artists · September 14, 2026 · 4 min read

Key takeaways
  • A growing share of companies hold the holiday party in January, and venues have followed, with several Denver-area holiday offers now running into the third week of January and past it.
  • In January, your party may be the only invitation your people have that week. In December it competes with everyone else's.
  • You don’t have to change the music for January. Standards, covers and dance songs aren’t seasonal, so you can book the same acts in January that you’d book in December.
  • Ideas that need everyone’s attention, like live band karaoke, tend to go over better in January than in December.

If you’ve ever tried to get the whole office to a party in December, you know how much else is on everyone’s calendar. Planners who’ve moved the party to January tend to give the same two reasons. The first is that December is packed, so your party ends up competing with other invitations, school concerts and flights home. The second is that the person planning the party spends December working on it and misses out on everything else. If you move the date past the new year, your party may be the only thing on people’s calendars that week, venues tend to have more dates open, and you get to enjoy the party you organized.

Denver’s venues have noticed this too. Several now run their holiday packages from the middle of November into the third week of January and past it, and they sell those January dates the same way they sell December. So a January party is now just part of the holiday season.

The January holiday party #

This is the party you’d have held in December, just moved to the second or third week of January. You can keep the same kind of venue, the same bar and the same speeches, and you may find that more of your people can make it. You can book the music just as you would in December, with a duo during drinks and dinner and then a DJ or a band for the dancing, all on one contract. We have more ideas for holiday party music, whether your date is in December or January.

The kickoff dinner with live music #

Many sales teams get together for a kickoff in January, and the dinner after a day of sessions can easily double as the company party. A jazz trio is a great choice for that dinner, because your team can talk easily over the music after a long day in meetings. If you’d like dancing after dinner, we can bring in a band or a DJ for that part too.

The live band karaoke night #

This is our favorite idea for January. Live band karaoke works best when everyone’s paying attention, and that’s a lot easier to get when your party isn’t competing with the holidays. A live band plays 80s and 90s hits, and anyone who wants to sing can grab the mic for a song. The lyrics show up on a screen, and a host keeps things moving. You can learn more about our live band karaoke and the band sizes we offer.

The awards-and-dance night #

This one is a look back at the year, followed by awards and then dancing. The band can keep the volume low through dinner, stop for the remarks and each award, and then get people dancing as soon as the emcee wraps up. A soul and R&B band or our yacht rock band is a great choice for the dancing. Want to know how the band follows the program? Learn more about music for galas and award nights.

The low-key dinner with solo piano #

This one is for the company that wants January to feel like a reset. It’s a private dinner with a solo pianist playing standards and covers as people arrive and while they eat, at a volume where everyone can talk. One pianist is about as simple as live music gets, and it can make a dinner feel like a real occasion without any kind of program.

The apres-ski evening #

If you’re holding your party at a venue in Vail, Beaver Creek or Breckenridge in January, it’s already an apres-ski party, and you won’t need any more of a theme than the snow. Our Americana band plays Colorado favorites, which makes it an easy pick for a mountain party. Need to think through the drive and lodging? Learn more about mountain retreats.

January work party themes #

Winter white is an easy one, since many venues already look the part in January. An apres-ski theme works in the city or in the mountains. You could also build the party around the year in review and give out awards along the way. A belated New Year’s party is a fun option for an office that couldn’t get everyone together on the thirty-first. The simplest option is no theme at all, just a lovely dinner and a great band.
