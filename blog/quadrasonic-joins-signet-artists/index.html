Quadrasonic brings Signet Artists to Vail

Quadrasonic, a four-piece rock band that lives in the Vail Valley, is now one of our Featured Artists, so you can book a full band from right there in Vail.

Signet Artists · November 12, 2026 · 2 min read

We’re excited to share that Quadrasonic is now one of our Featured Artists, and they bring Signet to Vail! They’re a four-piece alternative rock band that lives in the Vail Valley, with lead vocals, electric guitar, bass and drums.

If you’re getting married in Vail or Beaver Creek, or bringing your team to Vail for a retreat, you can now book a full band from right there in the valley.

Quadrasonic playing Pat Benatar’s Hit Me With Your Best Shot.

Where Quadrasonic plays #

The band plays weddings, festivals, summer concert series and clubs all over Colorado, including the GoPro Mountain Games in Vail and Copper Mountain’s summer concert series. In 2025 the Vail Daily introduced them to Eagle County as a new alternative rock band. Their singer has been on stage her whole life, and she can go from a grunge growl to soaring high notes.

What Quadrasonic plays #

Their song list runs from the ’70s to today, with a lot of ’90s alternative radio in the middle. You’ll hear Pat Benatar, Blondie and Joan Jett alongside Nirvana, No Doubt, The Cranberries and Garbage, and newer songs from Paramore and Chappell Roan. They play a few pop hits too, like Toxic and Levitating, with the guitars turned up.

Booking Quadrasonic for your event #

Quadrasonic plays as a four-piece band, and they’re a great choice when you want people dancing at a wedding reception, a big birthday or a company party. If your team is meeting in Vail, they’re an easy choice for your closing party, too.

Want to hear more? Check out Quadrasonic’s video clips and song list.

Want to book Quadrasonic for your event?

Just send us your date and venue, and we’ll let you know if they’re available.
