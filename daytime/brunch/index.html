Jazz brunch in Denver

Live music for a Sunday brunch.

There’s nothing quite like a slow Sunday brunch with a little live jazz in the corner. We can bring that sound to your restaurant, hotel or club every week, or to a single family brunch. Either way, you can choose a duo, a solo guitarist or a pianist.

Who books it

Brunch music for restaurants, hotels and families.

Restaurants, hotels and clubs
We can play every week or once a month, and if you have a piano, we can bring a pianist, too. We’ll put the whole run on one contract. Learn more about our weekly live music program.
The morning after a wedding
A duo or a solo guitarist can play your post-wedding brunch, whether it’s at the hotel or at home. If we played your cocktail hour, we can often send the same duo back for some of the weekend’s favorite songs. You can book the wedding and the brunch together, too.
A bridal brunch, a shower or a birthday
A guitarist or a Spanish guitarist is a lovely way to make a brunch feel special. A singer with a guitar can add the songs the guest of honor asks for, in a private dining space, a garden or at home. Find more ideas for showers and private parties.
A client or member brunch
A pianist or a duo playing jazz standards is a great fit for a hotel member event, a company family day or a club’s Mother’s Day brunch. Think of it as a daytime client dinner.

The song list

What do musicians play at a jazz brunch?

Our duo plays jazz standards and bossa novas, including Autumn Leaves, The Girl from Ipanema, All of Me, Blue Bossa and Someday My Prince Will Come, and we’ll keep the song list relaxed enough for a Sunday morning. A solo guitarist can play the same songs, a Spanish guitarist is a lovely choice for a patio, and a singer with a guitar leans more toward folk and singer-songwriter songs.

What clients say

Questions

What people ask about live brunch music.

Can a restaurant or hotel book live music for brunch every Sunday?
Yes. You’ll have one personal contact for the whole run, and we’ll do our best to send the same musicians each time. Just tell us your seating times and a little about the space, and we’ll get back to you with a recommendation and a price. We can bring brunch musicians to restaurants and hotels all over Denver.
How loud is brunch music?
We’ll keep it around the level of conversation at the tables, so nobody has to lean in to order. Sets usually run 45 to 60 minutes, and we can keep music playing through the breaks so the dining space doesn’t go quiet.
What does a dining space need?
We’ll need a corner of about 8 by 6 feet for a duo or 6 by 4 for a solo musician, plus one outlet. Setup can take 45 minutes to an hour, so we’ll be ready before your first seating, and packing up can take 30 to 45 minutes after the last one. On a patio, we’ll need a covered, level and dry spot with power, or we can use a battery-powered system, and we’ll set a time to make the call on moving inside. We can send a certificate of insurance and whatever else the venue asks for.

Send us your date and seating times, and we’ll help you find the right music for your brunch.
