Your options

A solo pianist, with a singer if you’d like one.

The piano
When the venue has a piano, the pianist plays it, and a hotel lobby or a club dining space usually has one. If it doesn’t, we’ll bring a stage piano (a portable electric piano), and from the tables it sounds a lot like an acoustic piano.
With a singer
On its own, the piano is instrumental, and that’s what most people choose for a client dinner or a gala. If you’d like some singing, we can add a singer for a set or for the whole event.

For a bigger crowd, we can bring a jazz duo for the same hours. Want people dancing after dinner? We can have a band or our DJ take over, and it can all stay on the same contract.

Pianist questions

Wondering about something else? Our full question list has answers on everything from substitutions to volume limits.

What does the pianist play?
Our pianists play standards and covers, from the Great American Songbook to Elton John and Billy Joel. We can lean toward jazz or pop, or move between the two as your event goes on.
How loud is it?
It’s about as loud as the conversation at the table, so nobody has to raise their voice and everyone can still hear the piano. Your pianist can adjust to the space, too, playing very softly in a small dining area and using a little more sound in a ballroom so the back tables can hear without it getting loud up front.
Can we ask for a song, or add time?
Yes. If you want a song that isn't on the list, let us know when you book and we can usually learn it, and if you think the dinner might run long, let us know that too. We can also bring a microphone for any toasts or remarks.
