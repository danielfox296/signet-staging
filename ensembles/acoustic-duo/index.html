Sizes

Which acoustic duo or trio is right for you?

Voice and guitar
A singer and a guitarist are a great fit for cocktail hours, welcome drinks and patios, and they can keep familiar songs just loud enough to feel like live music without taking over the conversation.
Guitar and upright bass
This is our instrumental duo, and it’s a lovely choice for dinner. The melodies are still familiar, and without lyrics the music can sit under the conversation at the table.
The trio: voice, guitar and upright bass
With a trio, you get a fuller sound for a bigger crowd, and the musicians can play one set with vocals and one without.

Need just one musician? Our singer and guitarist is one person who sings and plays guitar. Want your event to end on the dance floor? Tejas Singh can start as a duo and grow to a quartet.

Acoustic duo and trio questions

Have another question? Take a look at our full list of questions.

How loud is it?
It’s about as loud as the conversation around it. You’ll hear that it’s live music from across the venue, and the people at the nearest table can still talk easily. The musicians set their volume to fit your space, and they’re happy to adjust it.
How long can the duo play?
The duo usually plays 45 to 60 minutes at a time with 15 to 20 minutes between sets, and we can keep music playing through the breaks. A single hour works well, and so does playing from the time guests arrive through dessert. If you think the night might run long, let us know when you book.
Can the acoustic duo play outside?
Yes, and it’s a great fit for a patio. We’ll just need a dry, level spot under cover with power nearby, and we’ll size the sound system to your space. Colorado evenings cool down fast, so we’ll work out a plan with you for moving indoors.
