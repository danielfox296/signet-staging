Song list

What does a funk band play?

The band plays James Brown, the Meters, Stevie Wonder, Herbie Hancock, Sly and the Family Stone, Prince, Earth, Wind & Fire and the funk side of Motown, just to name a few. Your guests will likely recognize a lot of these songs from the bass line alone, and that's what the band leans into.

The band can play slower, warmer songs during dinner and pick up the pace when it's time to dance. Want a singer? Our soul and R&B band plays the same songwriters with a singer out front and a horn on the choruses.

Sizes

Do you need a funk trio or a quartet?

The trio
The trio is guitar, bass and drums. It's a great size when the dance floor is close to the tables, like in a club, a restaurant or a backyard.
The quartet
The quartet adds a horn or keys for a bigger sound, which is great in a ballroom or with a crowd of more than two hundred. A horn can play the horn parts from the original songs, and keys can fill out the sound under the guitar solos.

Want something mellower for cocktails? A jazz duo or a solo guitarist can play before the band, and a DJ can keep the party going afterward.

Funk band questions

Have a question we haven't covered? Our full FAQ goes into more, or you can just ask us.

How loud is a funk band?
While people are eating, the band can keep the drums light and the volume down, so conversation is easy. Once the dancing starts, the band can turn it up, and they'll keep the volume comfortable for the guests sitting closest. The band will adjust to the venue and the crowd, and if there's a sound limit, just tell us the number so they can stay under it.
Can a funk band play outdoors?
Yes, the band plays a lot of patios and lawns. The band just needs a covered, level, dry spot with power, and we'll work out a backup plan with you, since Colorado weather can turn fast.
Can we add time or ask for a song?
Yes. Just tell us the song when you book, and the band can usually learn it. If you think your event might run long, let us know that too.

Planning a party and looking for a funk band? Send us the date and we'll see what we can do.
