One voice and a guitar

A singer and guitarist for hire in Denver for cocktail hours, patios and small weddings.

Few things loosen up a cocktail hour like someone with a guitar singing Brown Eyed Girl. It’s one of the most popular choices we book in Denver, because one musician can sing the songs your guests know by heart and fit in just about any corner.

Song list

What songs does a singer and guitarist play?

You’ll hear singer-songwriter favorites, pop and soul from the last sixty years, and the acoustic side of classic rock, like Tom Petty, Bill Withers and Beatles ballads. These songs sound great with just one voice and one guitar, since the guitar can cover the rest of the record.

We can keep things mellow or pick up the pace, so just tell us how you’d like your event to feel. If you want something that isn’t on the list, like a first dance or a processional song, let us know when you book and we can usually learn it.

Who plays it

Choose any of our singers, or ask for one by name.

The singer and guitarist
We’ll match you with one of our singers based on your date and your guests. This is a great way to go if the sound and the date matter more to you than the name.
Tony Medina
Tony has a smoky voice and plays guitar with the rhythm of a funk player. He plays blues and classic rock, either solo for welcome drinks and dinner or as a band of up to four if you want people dancing. Want to see him play? Check out his video clips.
René Moffatt
René is a songwriter with his own folk and Americana songs, and he sings and plays acoustic guitar. He can mix in covers if you’d like, and he plays solo for a ceremony, a cocktail hour or a dinner, or with his band of up to four. You can watch video clips on his page.
Tejas Singh
Tejas is a singer-songwriter who builds familiar songs from the ground up with live looping (recording and layering parts on the spot). He fronts a duo that can grow into a quartet. He’s an excellent choice if you want to start small and finish on a dance floor.

Looking for instrumental guitar instead? Our solo guitarist plays jazz standards and Spanish-tinged pieces.

A singer and guitarist is a lovely way to fill the hour after the ceremony or the welcome drinks the night before, with songs both families know. The same musician can play the ceremony first, with an instrumental prelude and processional, and we can run the officiant’s microphone, too.

Singer and guitarist questions

Still have a question? You’ll find more in our full question list, from substitutions to volume limits.

Is one singer with a guitar enough for a cocktail hour?
For most cocktail hours and patios, yes. One voice and a guitar can carry across a patio and still sound like real live music. Above about a hundred guests spread across a ballroom, our acoustic duo adds a second musician to fill the space.
How loud is it?
It’s soft enough that your guests can keep talking, and your singer can always adjust to the space.
How long are the sets?
Sets usually run 45 to 60 minutes, and we can keep music going through the breaks. You can book a single hour, or have music from arrival through dinner. If you think your event might run long, let us know when you book.
Can a singer and guitarist play outdoors?
Yes, we play patios and lawns all the time. We’ll need a covered, level, dry spot with power nearby, and we’ll work out a plan with you for moving indoors, since Colorado evenings cool off fast and it’s hard to sing and play in the cold.
