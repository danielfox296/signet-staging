What does a millennial party band play?

Millennial party rock means the big rock and pop songs of the 2000s, from The Killers, Blink-182, Fall Out Boy and Paramore to Kelly Clarkson and Outkast.

Send us the songs you loved back then, and we’ll put the song list together with you.

Millennial party rock band questions

Something else on your mind? Take a look at our planner questions, from how far ahead to book to how loud a band plays.

How many musicians are in the band?
The band is a four-piece, with two guitars, bass and drums.
How much does a party band cost in Denver?
We quote each date one by one, since the cost depends on the day, how long you’d like the band to play and where your event is. Our pricing page gives a general view of what live music costs in Denver.
Can the band play our first dance?
In most cases, yes. Tell us the song when you book and we’ll check with the band, and we can plan the last song of the night with you, too.

Planning a party for people who grew up on the 2000s? Send us the date, and we’ll reply with the band’s availability and costs.

We’re happy to help with the rest of your event’s music, too.
