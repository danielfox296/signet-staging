Band & DJ

Adding a DJ after the live band

Already booking a band? It’s easy to add a DJ to keep the dancing going after the band finishes. When the band’s last set ends, the DJ can pick up through the same sound system, so the music doesn’t stop.

For a Denver holiday party, many hosts book a duo for cocktails and dinner and a DJ for the rest of the event, and we’ll plan the handoff with you when we go over the flow of the evening. Hiring a DJ on their own, with production lighting, can run $2,500 to $6,000 in the Denver, Colorado market.

DJ plus live instrument

Add a live bass or saxophone player

A musician on bass or saxophone can play along with the DJ, so your guests get a live performance to watch, too. It’s a great way to keep people dancing late at a wedding or a company party, so just let us know if you’d like it.

The music

Can I choose what the DJ plays?

Yes, as much as you’d like. You can build the list yourself, give us a do-not-play list and leave the rest to the DJ, or just tell us about your crowd and let the DJ take it from there.

Open format means the DJ plays all kinds of music, so you can mix as many styles as you’d like.

DJ booking questions

Have a question about curfews or volume limits? You can find more answers on our full question list.

How loud is it?
It can be as loud as you want, up to what your venue allows. During dinner or a toast, the DJ can keep it low enough for easy talking, and then turn it up once people are dancing. If your venue has a sound limit, let us know and the DJ will work within it.
How late can the DJ play?
As late as the venue allows. If you think your party might run long, just let us know when you book.
Does the DJ take requests?
Yes. The DJ will play your requests, and guests’ requests when they fit. We’d suggest spending a little time on your do-not-play list, and if a guest asks for something that might clear the dance floor, the DJ can suggest something else.
