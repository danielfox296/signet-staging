How it works

What does a musician DJ do?

The performer plays a full DJ set on their own decks, mixer and sound system. A few times through the evening, they pick up their instrument and play live over the mix for fifteen to thirty minutes, then go back to the decks.

Each performer plays one instrument, such as saxophone, violin, guitar, keys or hand drums, and we match you with the one you’d like to hear. The performer plays live for about a third of your event. The rest of the time, you have a DJ set with your requests and your do-not-play list.

The music

Can I choose what’s played live?

Yes. Give us a few songs you’d love to hear on the instrument, and the performer will plan the live passages around them. If there’s a song they don’t play yet, they can learn it for you.

The DJ set is open format, so the performer plays all kinds of music, and you can mix as many styles as you’d like.

Musician DJ questions

Have a question about curfews or volume limits? You can find more answers on our full question list.

What instrument does the performer play?
Each performer plays one instrument, such as saxophone, violin, guitar, keys or hand drums. Tell us what you’d like to hear, and we’ll match you with the right performer.
How loud is it?
As loud as your venue allows. The performer keeps the music low during dinner so your guests can talk, then turns it up for dancing. If your venue has a sound limit, let us know and the performer will work within it.
Does the musician DJ take requests?
Yes. The performer plays your requests, and guests’ requests when they fit, and works from your do-not-play list.
How much space does the performer need?
About the same as a DJ: a table, power nearby and a little space to stand and play. So a musician DJ is a good choice for a venue where a band won’t fit.
