Songs & styles

Should your solo guitarist sing or just play?

If you’d like vocals, the guitarist can sing blues, roots and singer-songwriter favorites, which usually works well for a cocktail hour or a patio. For guitar alone, we’d suggest jazz standards like Blue Bossa or some Spanish guitar pieces, and those are great during dinner. If you know you want that Spanish sound, take a look at our Spanish guitarist, and if singing is the main thing, check out our singer and guitarist.

One musician with a small sound system can fit into a lot of places, like a private dining space at a restaurant, an art gallery or a home where you’d like the furniture to stay put. For a bigger guest list, you may want two musicians, and our acoustic duo and jazz duo are both great options.

Solo guitarist questions

We’ve also put together a longer list of questions that come up a lot.

Is one guitarist enough for a cocktail hour?
Yes, for a shorter guest list, one guitarist is usually plenty. You'll usually be able to hear the guitar from anywhere in the space, even with everyone talking.
Who will be playing at our event?
We'll match you with a guitarist from our roster who suits the music you want, and we'll tell you who's playing when you book.
Can we ask for a song?
Yes. You can pick your favorites from the song list (and give us a do-not-play list, too). If you'd like a song that isn't on it, let us know when you book and we can usually learn it. If you think your event might run long, tell us that too, and we can plan a few more songs.
Can the solo guitarist play outdoors?
Yes, our guitarists often play on patios and in courtyards. You'll want a spot that's covered, level and dry, and if there's no power nearby, we can bring a battery-powered sound system. We'll also plan with you ahead of time for moving indoors, since Colorado evenings can turn cold fast.

Need a guitarist for your dinner or cocktail hour? Just send us the date and we'll get back to you with availability and a price.

Whatever you book, from a solo musician to a full band, you’ll have a personal point of contact for your event, and if we bring a sound system, we’ll size it to your venue.
