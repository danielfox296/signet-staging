What does a yacht rock band play?

This band plays songs like What a Fool Believes, Reelin’ in the Years and Rich Girl. That covers the Doobie Brothers, Steely Dan, Hall and Oates, Toto, Christopher Cross, Boz Scaggs, Kenny Loggins, Ambrosia, Player and Michael McDonald on his own, plus some of the Fleetwood Mac ballads.

The band can play ballads through dinner, and the up-tempo songs are great for getting people dancing.

Sizes

How many musicians do you need for a yacht rock band?

The four-piece
This band has keys, guitar, bass and drums, with three of the musicians singing. The harmonies are what make it yacht rock, so we keep all three voices at every size.
Five to six piece
This is the four-piece plus saxophone, with a lead singer of its own, and it’s a great fit for a ballroom or a crowd past two hundred. This is the size that sounds most like the records.

Want music before the band starts? An acoustic duo or solo pianist can play the cocktail hour on the same contract, and our DJ can keep things going once the band finishes.

Yacht rock band questions

Have another question? Take a look at our full list of questions for things like substitutions and volume limits. Curious where the name came from? Learn more about what yacht rock is.

What is a yacht rock band?
It’s a band that plays the smooth pop and soft rock of the late-70s and early-80s, with lots of harmony vocals and saxophone. If you’re looking for one in Denver, just reach out to us at Signet Artists.
How loud is it?
During dinner, the band can play softly enough that your guests can still talk. Once the dancing starts, it can sound like a full party band, whether you’re on a lawn or in a ballroom. The band will adjust the volume to suit the space and your crowd, and if your venue has a sound limit, tell us the number and we’ll work with you to stay under it.
Can it play outdoors?
Yes, and this band sounds great outdoors. The band will just need a covered, level, dry spot with power, and we’ll plan ahead with you for moving indoors, since Colorado weather can turn fast in the afternoon.
Can we add time or ask for a song?
Yes. Let us know the song when you book and the band can usually learn it (a few weeks' notice helps). If your event might go long, let us know that, too.
