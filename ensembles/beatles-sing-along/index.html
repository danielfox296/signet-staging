Beatles songs, and everybody sings

Your Bird Can Sing

A Beatles sing-along led by two musicians, for everyone from five to ninety.

You know the part of Hey Jude where everyone starts singing. A Beatles sing-along is a playful hour of that. Two musicians play and lead the singing, and by the second song that’s your whole crowd.

It books for a fiftieth anniversary or an eightieth birthday, a company picnic or holiday party, a wedding welcome party, a school or library program, and an afternoon at a senior living community. Every crowd in that list already knows these songs.

Occasions

Perfect booking for

Family parties
An anniversary, a milestone birthday, a retirement send-off or a backyard party, at the house or at a rented space. Whoever the party is for grew up on these records, and their grandchildren end up singing them too.
Company events
A summer picnic, the holiday party or the last night of a retreat. It’s the one thing the whole company does together, and it plays at a volume the tables can still talk across.
Weddings
A welcome party, a rehearsal dinner or the after-party. The two families meet for the first time that weekend, and they all know these songs, so it’s the first thing they do together.
Community events
A library’s summer program, a school’s family night, a church picnic or a block party. It’s open to whoever walks up, so the crowd is every age at once, and it’s one of the few programs that works for toddlers and retirees in the same hour.
Senior living and memory care
The residents know every one of these songs from the first time around. In memory care the same duo plays a shorter, quieter set to a smaller group.

Your Bird Can Sing questions

The questions every booking shares are on the full question list.

What is Your Bird Can Sing?
A Beatles sing-along led by two musicians, booked through Signet Artists in Denver, for family parties, company events, weddings, community programs and senior living.
Is this a Beatles tribute band?
No. Two musicians play the Beatles songbook as themselves and lead the audience through the songs.
Does anyone need to know the words?
No. Everyone gets a lyric booklet and the duo leads the melody, so anyone who wants to sing can, and anyone who would rather sit and listen is welcome to.
How long is it?
One set of 45 to 60 minutes, or two shorter sets with a break. Requests are welcome.
How loud is it?
About as loud as a conversation. People at the back hear the melody and the front table can keep talking. The duo sets the volume to the space and the crowd, so in a small space it plays with no amplification at all, and in a courtyard it plays louder to reach the back.
Can we book it every month?
Yes. It books as a standing date, the same afternoon each month or every other week, on one contract with one personal contact for the whole run, and the set changes from visit to visit.
Do you have a certificate of insurance and a W-9?
Yes. Both come in the same email as the contract, with an invoice that matches your purchase order, so a business office can set the vendor up before the date.
