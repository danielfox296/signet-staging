Christmas songs & jazz standards

A Christmas jazz trio for holiday parties and December dinners.

You know White Christmas the second it starts. This is the act to book when you want the Christmas songs everyone knows at your holiday party, played as jazz. The trio plays them on guitar, upright bass and drums, along with a few jazz standards that have nothing to do with Christmas.

It’s a lovely choice for a company holiday party, a client dinner, a member event at a club or a Christmas party at home.

Song list

What does a Christmas jazz trio play?

A holiday party can run a few hours, so the song list mixes two kinds of music.

Christmas standards
These are the holiday songs everyone knows, like White Christmas, The Christmas Song, Let It Snow, Have Yourself a Merry Little Christmas and Winter Wonderland, played in a jazz style.
Jazz standards
The trio also mixes in jazz standards like Autumn Leaves, Fly Me to the Moon and The Girl from Ipanema, which keeps the music from feeling like one long medley.

Let us know how much Christmas you want in the music. Some hosts love carols from start to finish, some like a single Christmas set, and many leave it to the trio to find the right mix.

Sizes

Solo guitarist, duo, trio or quartet for your holiday party

Solo
A solo jazz guitarist is a lovely fit for a client dinner or a party at home.
Duo
Upright bass and guitar are a great pair for a cocktail hour or a longer dinner when you want people talking. The duo can fit in a corner.
Trio
The trio adds drums played softly with brushes, so it’s a great size for a company party in a ballroom. Looking for a trio you can get to know by name? Check out Changes Jazz Trio.
Quartet

Christmas jazz trio questions

Have another question? Take a look at our full question list.

Is a Christmas jazz trio too quiet for a holiday party?
Not for dinner, when quiet is usually what you want. With brushes on the drums, your guests can talk easily and still hear The Christmas Song. If you’d like dancing, too, just let us know and we can plan for that.
How far ahead should we book a Christmas jazz trio?
Whenever you're ready. Just tell us the date and the venue, and we’ll check the booking calendar and reply with a recommendation and costs. If your party is coming up sooner, no worries. We can often make something great work.
Does the Christmas jazz trio play New Year's Eve?
Yes. The trio can leave the carols behind and play jazz standards and swing, so just let us know what you have in mind.
Can we ask for a particular song?
Yes. Let us know the song when you book and we can usually learn it.
