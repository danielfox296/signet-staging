Live music at the convention center hotel

Hyatt Regency Denver at Colorado Convention Center

Hyatt Regency Denver is the big hotel connected to the Colorado Convention Center, with 1,100 guest rooms and the largest hotel ballroom in the state.

A lot of the conference receptions, awards dinners and company parties in Denver happen here, along with big weddings, and the rooftop terrace looks out over the skyline.

Event spaces at Hyatt Regency Denver

Centennial Ballroom
The Centennial Ballroom is about 30,000 sq ft with 30-foot ceilings, for banquets of up to about 1,900 guests and receptions of up to 3,000. It divides into smaller sections, and the Centennial Foyer outside it holds receptions of up to 1,500.
Capitol Ballroom
The Capitol Ballroom has 22-foot ceilings and hand-blown Italian glass light fixtures, for seated events of up to about 1,200.
Denver City Terrace
The Denver City Terrace is a rooftop with views of the skyline, about 3,500 sq ft, for up to 300 guests. The hotel holds wedding ceremonies up there.
Silver Room
The Silver Room is about 1,200 sq ft with floor-to-ceiling windows, for rehearsal dinners and small receptions.

When a big convention is in town, the hotel and the streets around it are busy, so we build in extra time for the act to arrive and set up.
