Live music at a Downtown Denver hotel

Hilton Denver City Center

Hilton Denver City Center is on California Street, a short walk from the Colorado Convention Center, with 613 guest rooms and three ballrooms.

It’s a busy conference and company-meeting hotel, and it hosts weddings too, with its own wedding planning team.

Event spaces at Hilton Denver City Center

Colorado Ballroom
The Colorado Ballroom is the hotel’s biggest, for events of up to about 1,200 guests, and it divides into eight sections.
Denver Ballroom
The Denver Ballroom is about 5,900 sq ft, for banquets of up to 450. It divides into six salons, and it opens onto a pre-function space of about 3,200 sq ft.
Penrose Ballroom
The Penrose Ballroom is about 2,800 sq ft, for dinners of up to about 200 and receptions of up to 240, across from Prospect’s Urban Kitchen and Bar.
Outdoor plaza
The hotel has an outdoor plaza that it uses for weddings and receptions.
