Wedding and event music at a Boulder hotel

Hotel Boulderado

Hotel Boulderado opened with a New Year’s Eve ball in 1909, a block from Pearl Street, and couples still get married on its Grand Staircase under the stained-glass ceiling.

Live music is part of the hotel already. There’s jazz in the lobby on Friday evenings, and music every week downstairs at License No. 1.

Event spaces at Hotel Boulderado

Mezzanine and the Grand Staircase

The Mezzanine Lounge is about 3,300 sq ft under the stained-glass ceiling, for dinners of up to 80 and receptions of up to 110. Ceremonies happen on the Grand Staircase, with up to 180 guests watching from the Mezzanine.

Our pick

Classical Strings

A string quartet is a magical fit for a ceremony on the staircase.

Boulderado Ballroom

The Boulderado Ballroom is about 2,900 sq ft, for dinners of up to 180 and receptions of up to 250. The ceiling is lower than most ballrooms, at about 8 ft 8 in.

Our pick

Tejas Singh

With that lower ceiling, Tejas as a duo or a trio suits the ballroom better than a big band.

Columbine-Balsam

Columbine-Balsam is about 2,200 sq ft, for dinners of up to 120 and receptions of up to 175, and Columbine on its own holds dinners of up to 80.

Our pick

Jazz Duo

A jazz duo for a dinner of up to 120 in Columbine-Balsam.

Spruce and the Corner Bar

Spruce Farm & Fish and the Corner Bar each host private brunches and dinners of up to 30.

Our pick

Solo Guitarist

A solo guitarist for a private brunch or dinner of up to 30 at Spruce or the Corner Bar.

Planning music at Hotel Boulderado

Sound system
The ballroom’s ceiling is low, so we size the band and the sound to fit it.
Catering
The hotel caters every event in-house, and its wedding packages include a dance floor.
The hotel’s own music
There’s jazz in the lobby on Friday evenings, so we’ll plan your music around it.
The holidays
The hotel lights a 29-foot Christmas tree in the lobby each year, and it hosts holiday teas and buffets.
Parking
Parking at the hotel is valet only, with street parking nearby.

Questions about music at Hotel Boulderado

How much does live music add to a Hotel Boulderado wedding?
It depends on the act and how much of the day you’d like covered, from a violinist on the Grand Staircase to a trio in the ballroom. For the Colorado market in general, check out our pricing page. For your date, just send it over and we’ll quote it.
