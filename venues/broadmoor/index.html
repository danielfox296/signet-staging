Wedding and event music at a Colorado Springs resort

The Broadmoor

The Broadmoor opened in 1918 on Cheyenne Lake, at the foot of Cheyenne Mountain, and it’s still the grandest resort in Colorado for a wedding, a gala or a company meeting.

It has over 200,000 sq ft of meeting and event space, from the original ballroom to a hall for thousands, and lodges up on the mountain.

Trusted and booked by

Event spaces at the Broadmoor

The Main Ballroom

The Main Ballroom dates back to the 1918 opening, with ornate ceilings and hardwood floors. It seats up to 240 for a banquet and holds 300 for a reception, and the resort holds wedding ceremonies here too.

Our pick

Classical Strings

A string quartet under those 1918 ceilings is a lovely way to start a wedding, and the same players can stay on through dinner.

The Lake Terrace Dining Room

The Lake Terrace Dining Room looks out over Cheyenne Lake and the mountains, with seating for up to 240 at a banquet.

Our pick

Jazz Duo

For a rehearsal dinner with the lake out the windows, upright bass and guitar keep things relaxed and easy to talk over.

The Lakeside Terrace

The Lakeside Terrace is the outdoor space beside the lake, for receptions of up to 300.

Our pick

Dirty Flamenco

Gary’s Spanish guitar outdoors by Cheyenne Lake is our favorite pick for a cocktail hour or a welcome reception.

Cheyenne Lodge

Cheyenne Lodge is a rustic lodge up on the mountain, with about 6,300 sq ft for banquets of up to 300 and receptions of up to 450.

Our pick

Meghan Clarisse

A rustic lodge up the mountain calls for country, and Meghan Clarisse can play it solo by the fireplace or bring her five-piece band.

Broadmoor Hall and the International Center

Broadmoor Hall is about 60,000 sq ft, for receptions of up to 4,600, and the resort holds weddings of over 1,000 guests there. The International Center is about 14,500 sq ft, for receptions of up to 1,500.

Our pick

Tejas Singh

Tejas can start as a duo for a conference reception and grow into a four-piece for the dancing, so one act covers the whole party.

Planning music at the Broadmoor

Power and staging
The resort’s wedding packages include power and a riser for the band or DJ, and a dance floor. Our acts bring their own sound system.
AV
INSPIRE is the resort’s AV company, for anything beyond what the act brings.
Paperwork
Every wedding has a day-of coordinator, and the resort asks for proof of insurance from the vendors. We’ll get it to them ahead of time.
Getting there
Cheyenne Lodge and the Mountain Clubhouse are up the mountain, away from the main resort, so we plan the drive and load-in with the events team.
Catering
The resort caters every event in-house.
Parking
There’s valet and self-parking at the resort.

Questions about music at the Broadmoor

Can we bring our own band to a Broadmoor wedding?
Yes. The resort’s wedding packages include power, a riser for the band or DJ and a dance floor, so bringing in your own band is part of how weddings work there. The resort asks its vendors for proof of insurance, and we can get ours to the events team ahead of time.
How much does live music add to a Broadmoor wedding?
It depends on how big the act is and how much of the day you’d like covered. A solo violin for the ceremony is a very different number from a six-piece band in Broadmoor Hall. Our pricing page has a general view of what live music costs in Colorado, and we’re happy to quote your date.
