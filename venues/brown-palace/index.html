Live music at a Downtown Denver hotel

The Brown Palace Hotel and Spa

The Brown Palace opened in 1892 with a banquet in its ballroom, and it’s still one of the most elegant places in Denver for a wedding, a gala or a holiday party.

Live music is already part of the hotel. There’s piano at afternoon tea in the atrium lobby, and jazz on Friday and Saturday evenings.

Event spaces at the Brown Palace

Grand Ballroom
The Grand Ballroom is the hotel’s historic ballroom, about 5,800 sq ft, for banquets of up to 400 guests and receptions of up to 480. It divides into two halves of about 2,900 sq ft each.
Onyx Room
The Onyx Room is a historic salon of about 1,425 sq ft, for up to 100 guests, with white onyx on the walls and a recreated medallion mural on the ceiling.
Central City
Central City is about 2,150 sq ft, for banquets of up to 90, and its look goes back to the gold rush.
Brown Palace Club
The Brown Palace Club is a long space, about 28 by 110 ft, for seated dinners of up to 80. Eisenhower ran his campaign from the hotel, and the club keeps that history.
Ellyngton’s and the 5280 Suite
Ellyngton’s is about 5,400 sq ft, for banquets of up to 150 and receptions of up to 250. The 5280 Suite is about 3,500 sq ft, for banquets of up to 220.
Atrium lobby
The atrium is the heart of the hotel, eight floors high under a stained-glass ceiling, with afternoon tea by day and cocktails in the evening.

Load-in and setup

Sound system
Our acts bring their own sound system, sized to the space. The hotel also has AV on site.
A historic building
It’s an 1892 building, so we work out load-in and staging with the hotel’s events team ahead of time.
Catering
The hotel caters events in-house, and wedding packages include a dance floor and a DJ table.

Things to plan around

The hotel’s own music
The atrium has jazz on Friday and Saturday evenings and piano at afternoon tea, so we’ll plan your music to fit around it.
The holidays
In the holiday season the atrium has carolers and the Champagne Cascade, a tower of over 5,000 champagne glasses.
Parking
There’s valet parking at the hotel.
