Wedding and event music at a Downtown Denver hotel

Four Seasons Hotel Denver

Four Seasons Hotel Denver is a 45-story tower a few blocks from the Denver Center for the Performing Arts, and its Grand Ballroom is one of the city’s favorite places for a wedding or a gala.

There’s a terrace by the pool on the third floor with views of the city, so you can have the cocktail hour outside and dinner in the ballroom.

Trusted and booked by

Event spaces at Four Seasons Hotel Denver

Grand Ballroom

The Grand Ballroom is about 5,200 sq ft, for banquets of up to 350 guests. It divides into two equal halves, and it has its own pre-function area.

Our pick

Little Moses Jones

Little Moses Jones, with 90s and 2000s R&B and hip-hop for the dance floor.

Cottonwood Ballroom

The Cottonwood Ballroom is the hotel’s junior ballroom, about 3,400 sq ft, and it divides into two.

Our pick

Tejas Singh

The junior ballroom is a lovely size for Tejas as a trio or a full band.

Pool Terrace

The Pool Terrace is an outdoor event space on the third floor with views of the city, for banquets of up to 200.

Our pick

Dirty Flamenco

Spanish guitar is hard to beat for cocktails on the terrace.

Private dining and smaller spaces

The hotel has three private dining spaces and seven smaller meeting spaces, for rehearsal dinners, client dinners and breakouts.

Our pick

Jazz Duo

For a rehearsal dinner or a client dinner in one of the private dining spaces, a jazz duo stays easy to talk over.

Planning music at Four Seasons Hotel Denver

Sound system
The Grand Ballroom has a stage and a sound system of its own, and the hotel’s team can tell you how the band connects to it.
Load-in
We plan the layout, load-in and load-out with the hotel’s events team, who sign off on them ahead of time.
Catering
The hotel caters events in-house.
Weather
The Pool Terrace is outdoors, so we’ll plan the music with an indoor backup if the weather turns.
Parking
There’s valet parking at the hotel.
