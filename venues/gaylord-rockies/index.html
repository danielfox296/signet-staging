Wedding and event music at an Aurora resort and convention center

Gaylord Rockies Resort & Convention Center

Gaylord Rockies is the biggest resort in Colorado for events, on 85 acres out by Denver International Airport. It has four ballrooms, almost 555,000 sq ft of meeting space and lawns that look west to the mountains.

Most events here are conferences, company meetings and big weddings, with guests flying in from all over. Live music works in every part of it, from a duo at the welcome reception to a string quartet at the ceremony and a full band in the ballroom.

Event spaces at Gaylord Rockies

Juniper Ballroom and Patio

The Juniper Ballroom is about 7,500 sq ft, for banquets of up to 400 guests and receptions of about 700. The Juniper Patio outside it is about 13,400 sq ft, and the resort uses the two together for weddings of up to 400.

Our pick

Tejas Singh

Tejas can cover both the patio and the ballroom, as a duo outside and a band inside.

Colorado and Adams ballrooms

The Colorado Ballroom and the Adams Ballroom are about 34,000 sq ft each, for banquets of up to about 2,000 guests and receptions of over 3,000. Each one has about 10,000 sq ft of pre-function space outside it.

Our pick

Little Moses Jones

A ballroom for thousands needs a band with real energy, and Little Moses Jones bring their own sound engineer, too.

Aurora Ballroom

The Aurora Ballroom is the biggest, at about 47,600 sq ft, for banquets of up to about 2,550 guests. It sits beside the Aurora Exhibit Hall, which is about 175,000 sq ft.

Our pick

Quadrasonic

Quadrasonic’s rock covers and powerhouse singer, for a conference party in the biggest ballroom at the resort.

Mountain View Pavilion

The Mountain View Pavilion faces west to the mountains, with about 9,500 sq ft indoors for banquets of up to 450 and receptions of up to 800. Around it are patios, terraces and the Longs Peak, Mount Blue Sky and Pikes Peak lawns.

Our pick

Classical Strings

A beautiful spot for strings at a wedding.

Grand Lodge and its lawn

The Grand Lodge is the resort’s big central lounge, and the resort has live music there most evenings. The Grand Lodge Lawn outside has four firepits and two fireplaces.

Our pick

Meghan Clarisse

Meghan Clarisse, solo or as a duo, for a gathering out on the lawn.

Front Range Lawn

The Front Range Lawn is about 39,800 sq ft, for outdoor events of up to 3,600 guests.

Our pick

Tony Medina

Tony Medina’s four-piece for an outdoor party on the Front Range Lawn, with blues and rock and roll from Freddie King to Prince.

Planning music at Gaylord Rockies

AV and power
Encore is the resort’s in-house AV company, and it handles AV and power in the ballrooms, the Grand Lodge and the restaurants. Our acts bring their own sound system, and wedding packages at the resort include power for a band or DJ.
Load-in
With almost 555,000 sq ft of meeting space, we plan load-in with the resort’s events team, from the loading dock to the right ballroom.
Paperwork
The resort asks for the act’s details and a certificate of insurance ahead of the event, and we’ll get both to the events team in plenty of time.
Sound limits
In the Grand Lodge, music goes through the house sound system and stays under 95 dB, and entertainment there ends at 10pm.
Weddings
The resort caters every wedding in-house, and every wedding has an approved day-of coordinator. Outside musicians are welcome, and the ceremony package includes a weather backup location.
Parking
There’s self-parking and valet, and wedding guests get discounted self-parking.
