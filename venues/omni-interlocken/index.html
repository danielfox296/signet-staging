Live music at a Broomfield resort hotel

Omni Interlocken Hotel

Omni Interlocken sits on a 27-hole golf course between Denver and Boulder, with views of the Rockies and about 40,000 sq ft of indoor and outdoor event space.

It’s a favorite for company meetings from both cities, and for weddings with the ceremony on the lawn and the reception in the ballroom.

Event spaces at Omni Interlocken

Interlocken Ballroom
The Interlocken Ballroom is about 9,100 sq ft with 16-foot ceilings, for receptions of up to about 950 and weddings of up to 500 seated with a dance floor. It divides into four salons, and its pre-function space looks out over the golf course.
Centennial Ballroom
The Centennial Ballroom is about 4,100 sq ft, for receptions of up to about 420 and weddings of up to 170 with a dance floor. It splits into two equal halves.
Ponderosa
Ponderosa is about 1,600 sq ft, with French doors to the lobby atrium, for dinners of up to 70 with a dance floor.
Outdoor Pavilion
The Outdoor Pavilion is covered, about 4,750 sq ft, for banquets of up to 320 and receptions of up to 475, with golf course views, a waterfall and a fire pit. It’s open from late May to early October.
Ceremony lawn
The lawn holds ceremonies of up to 220 guests seated, looking out over the golf course.
