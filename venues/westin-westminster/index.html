Wedding and event music at a Westminster hotel

The Westin Westminster

The Westin Westminster has over 100,000 sq ft of event space off US 36, between Denver and Boulder, with three ballrooms and a string of courtyards, gardens and terraces outside them.

It hosts big conferences, company parties and weddings, including Indian weddings, which the hotel plans for.

Trusted and booked by

Event spaces at the Westin Westminster

Waterfall Gardens and the courtyards

The Waterfall Gardens are about 19,800 sq ft outdoors, for receptions of up to 400. The North and South courtyards hold receptions of up to 350 and 400.

Our pick

Classical Strings

A string quartet is a classic choice for a reception out in the gardens.

Westminster Ballroom

The Westminster Ballroom is about 11,900 sq ft with 22-foot ceilings, for banquets of up to 840 and receptions of up to 1,200. It divides into four sections.

Our pick

Little Moses Jones

Little Moses Jones for a big party in the Westminster Ballroom.

Legacy and Standley ballrooms

The Legacy Ballroom is about 9,600 sq ft, for banquets of up to 675 and receptions of up to 960. The Standley Ballroom is about 4,600 sq ft, for banquets of up to 320 and receptions of up to 500.

Our pick

Quadrasonic

Quadrasonic for a company party in the Legacy or Standley Ballroom.

The Lake House

The Lake House is about 3,300 sq ft, for banquets of up to 200 and receptions of up to 250.

Our pick

Tejas Singh

Tejas as a duo or a band for a wedding at the Lake House.

Sunset Rooftop Deck

The Sunset Rooftop Deck is about 2,700 sq ft, for banquets of up to 110 and receptions of up to 140.

Our pick

Dirty Flamenco

Spanish guitar on the Sunset Rooftop Deck, for cocktails up top.
