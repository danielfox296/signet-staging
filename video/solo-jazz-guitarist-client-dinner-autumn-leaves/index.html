Solo jazz guitarist for a client dinner: Autumn Leaves

This is Autumn Leaves on one guitar, quiet enough to talk over. A solo guitarist works well for a smaller group and only needs a small corner of your venue.

We can bring a solo guitarist to Denver, Boulder, Colorado Springs or the mountain towns. One musician fits into a lot of places, like a private dining space at a restaurant, an art gallery or a home, and our guitarists often play on patios and in courtyards, too.
