Dirty Flamenco plays Bart's rumba at a garden party, and they can bring the volume down so your guests can keep talking.

A dinner at home is one of the best fits for the trio, indoors or out in the backyard. They also play fiftieth and sixtieth birthdays, anniversaries and retirement parties, in Denver or up in the mountains.
