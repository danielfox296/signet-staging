Planning your party

Three things to know about a December party.

There are usually speeches. Somebody thanks the team, somebody hands out an award. We’ll have a microphone ready for your speakers, and the music will stop before they reach it.

Half the crowd wants to talk and the other half is waiting to dance. A duo through dinner and a DJ for the dancing can keep both groups happy.

The venue is usually a restaurant or a hotel ballroom. We’ll work out setup, power and any volume limit with the venue ahead of time, so you don’t have to.

Holiday party questions

Need stage sizes and power details for your venue? Check out our technical page. You’ll find more answers on our planner FAQ.

How loud is it?
We’ll keep the music at whatever level suits your party, up to what the venue allows, and we can bring it up or down as the party goes on.
What does the venue need to provide?
We’ll usually need a time at the loading dock and access to an elevator. Setup can take 45 to 90 minutes depending on the size of the act, and we may need dedicated power, too.
Can you go through our procurement process?
Yes. We can be set up as a vendor, quote against a purchase order and send whatever paperwork your finance team needs. Just let us know what the process looks like.

Holding it in January

The January holiday party.

Many Denver companies now hold the party in January, and the venues have followed, with holiday packages that run into the third week of January and past it.

All of this works just as well in January. Live band karaoke, the yacht rock band and Dirty Flamenco are all great choices for a January party, when your guests aren’t juggling as many invitations.
