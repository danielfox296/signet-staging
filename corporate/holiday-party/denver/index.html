The pairing

A duo and a DJ for your holiday party.

Many Denver holiday parties have two parts, a dinner where people want to talk and a dance floor for letting loose. A great way to cover both is to book a duo for cocktails and dinner and then a DJ to get people dancing, with one sound system and one contract for the whole party.

FAQ

Denver holiday party questions.

What does setup look like at a downtown Denver hotel?
The band will usually come in through the loading dock and the service elevator during the times your venue sets. The banquet team will want to know about space, power and timing first, so we’ll work all of that out with them ahead of time. A quartet will need more space, power and setup time than a duo. Catering warmers on the same circuit can cause trouble, so we’ll ask the venue for dedicated power. Signet Artists books company parties across Denver.
How loud is it?
It can be as quiet or as loud as you need. A duo can play softly enough for easy conversation, and a quartet or the DJ can go as big as the hotel allows.
Do December dates in Denver cost more?
Yes. December weekends usually cost more than a standard date across Colorado, and New Year's Eve is a peak date of its own. Send us the date and we'll come back with a price.
