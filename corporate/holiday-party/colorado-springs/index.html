Sizes

What size band do you need for a Springs holiday party?

A duo is a great choice for a company dinner, at a volume where everyone can keep talking. A quartet is the one to book when you want the whole ballroom dancing.

Many Springs company parties take place in a ballroom, with a few remarks from the stage and dancing once they’re done. You can book both, too, with the duo playing through dinner and the quartet taking over when the program ends.

Want one act for the whole party? Tejas Singh can start as a duo at dinner and grow to a quartet for the dancing, so you’ll hear the same singer all night, and it’s all on one contract.

Where the parties happen

Colorado Springs holiday party venues

The right live entertainment often depends on your venue, and the Springs has a few great kinds to choose from.

Resort ballrooms
The Broadmoor, Garden of the Gods Resort and Club, and Cheyenne Mountain Resort host many of the area’s biggest company parties. A quartet is often the right size for a ballroom this big, and it can play softly through dinner before getting people dancing.
Downtown venues
The Mining Exchange and the Antlers are great choices for a company dinner or a private floor, and they’re an easy walk if your office is downtown. A duo or a trio is a great fit for these, and either one can play quietly through dinner.
The restaurant buyout
When the whole company is sitting down together, we’ll keep the music under the conversation all night. A solo musician or a duo is the simplest way for a small company to celebrate the holidays.

FAQ

Colorado Springs booking questions.

Can you clear a defense contractor's vendor process?
Yes. Many companies in the Springs work with purchase orders and vendor onboarding, from defense contractors and firms around the Air Force Academy and the bases to national nonprofits based in town. We can be set up as a vendor and quote against a PO, and we can send whatever paperwork your finance team needs. Just let us know what the process looks like.
How loud is it?
That’s up to you. We can stay soft enough for easy conversation or play like a full dance band, as loud as the ballroom allows, and we’re happy to turn it up or down when you ask.
What do travel and setup look like?
Colorado Springs is a straightforward drive down the Front Range for us, so there’s no need to put anyone up overnight. Setup can take 45 to 90 minutes, and it mostly comes down to how big the band is. We’ll bring a sound system that’s right for your venue, and we’ll work out the loading dock and unloading with them ahead of time.
