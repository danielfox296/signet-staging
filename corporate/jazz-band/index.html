Your event

Jazz for each part of your corporate event.

You may want a different amount of music for each part of your event, and none at all during the program.

As the doors open
A duo of upright bass and guitar plays jazz standards as your guests arrive. You can tell it’s live from across the venue, and everyone can still talk easily.
Through dinner
A trio adds quiet drums, played with brushes, and it stays soft enough for everyone at the table to talk. Our Changes Jazz Trio is a great choice here if you’d like standards with a more modern edge.
The remarks and the awards
The band plays right up to the moment and then stops before your speaker reaches the microphone. We can also play each winner up to the stage. Check out our run of show planner for more.
After the program
When the program ends, the quartet gets people dancing. That’s the trio plus a horn or a singer, playing swing and soul favorites. If your event runs later, a DJ or a covers band can take over for the after-party.

Occasions

Corporate events that are a great fit for a jazz band.

Client dinners and member evenings
A duo can play the whole event, and it’s perfect when the conversation is what matters most.
Galas and award nights
A gala is a great place for a horn, so we often suggest the trio through dinner and the full quartet after the program.
Holiday parties
The trio can play drinks and dinner, and then the quartet with a singer or the DJ can get people dancing. In December the Christmas jazz trio plays the season’s songs.
Retreat evenings in the mountains
A duo is a lovely choice for a dinner at a mountain lodge.

Questions

What planners ask about corporate jazz bands.

Which size of jazz band does a corporate event need?
For a reception or a dinner for up to eighty guests, we’d suggest the duo or the trio. For a ballroom of two hundred, the trio through dinner and the quartet after the program make a great combination, with a horn or a singer out front. Tell us the headcount, the venue and whether the evening has a dance floor, and we'll recommend the size. Signet Artists books jazz bands across Denver and the Colorado mountains.
How loud is it?
We can play as softly as you’d like through the reception and dinner. Once the program ends, the quartet can play like a dance band, up to what the venue allows.
How long are the sets?
Sets can run 45 to 60 minutes, and we can keep music going through the breaks.
What do you need from us to quote it?
We just need the date, the venue and a rough idea of the flow of the evening. From there, we’ll recommend a size and send you a price, and we can share the space and power details with your venue.

Send us the date and we’ll help you find the right size of jazz band for your event.
