Galas and award nights

Gala entertainment in Denver

You've planned every part of the program, and the last thing you want is music playing over your emcee. We'll plan the music around your program with you. That can mean a reception act as the doors open, dinner music your guests can easily talk over, quiet for the emcee, and a dance floor after the last award.

We work with nonprofit galas and fundraisers, association award nights, chamber annual dinners and company award ceremonies.

From reception to dance floor

Live music for every part of your gala program

We'll go over the flow of the evening with whoever is running your program, and the bandleader will follow your emcee throughout the event.

Reception music
We can have a reception act playing when your first guests walk in, at a volume the check-in table can easily talk over.
Remarks and the welcome
The band can stop before your speaker reaches the podium, and we'll have a microphone ready for them.
Dinner
The band can play at the level of the conversation for the whole meal. A trio usually does this better than a full band turned down.
The video
If you're showing a video, the band will stop, we'll plan the switch to the house audio ahead of time, and the band can come back in after the applause.
The auction and the paddle raise
The band will stay quiet during the bidding unless your auctioneer would like some music, and we can play a short piece as each lot closes.
The walk-ups
We can play music for each honoree from the moment their name is read until they reach the podium, then stop as soon as they get there. We'll just need the names and the order ahead of time.
The dance floor
A dance band or DJ can take over without a long break to change things over.

Who plays each part

Gala bands, DJs & musicians

You can have the same musicians all night or three different acts. Either way, it can all go on one contract with one sound system.

As the doors open
Great choices include the jazz duo, a solo pianist, a Spanish guitarist, or a string quartet for a black-tie event. If you'd like something more modern, check out Last Consulate, with upright bass over slow, laid-back electronic music.
Through dinner
We'd suggest the jazz trio, with the drummer playing brushes to keep it soft, or the Americana trio for a gala at a ranch, a barn or up in the mountains.
After the last award
The soul and R&B band is great on a ballroom floor, the yacht rock band is perfect for a crowd that loves to sing, and live band karaoke lets your guests take the mic. You could also book the DJ on their own or with a live musician playing along.

Good to know

Gala and award night questions

Looking for stage sizes and power details? Check out our technical page. You can read more booking answers in our planner questions.

Do you play during the live auction and the paddle raise?
In our experience, most auctioneers prefer quiet during the bidding. The band will usually start again when the auctioneer hands the microphone back.
How loud can the band get?
Once the dancing starts, the band can turn it up as far as the venue allows. Let us know the venue's sound limit and curfew when you book and we'll plan the night around them.
What do you need from the venue?
If it’s a hotel, we'll need to know when we can use the loading dock and the elevators. We'll also need one or two dedicated 20-amp circuits, depending on the size of the act. We'd love to see the flow of the evening, too, including when your program needs the music to stop. We can send whatever paperwork your venue or your finance team needs.
Do you work with our emcee and auctioneer?
Yes. We'll have a microphone for your emcee and one for your auctioneer, and we'll go over the plan with both of them ahead of time. If your emcee is a board member doing it for the first time, we're glad to walk through it with them before the doors open.
How far ahead should we book entertainment for a gala?
Whenever you're ready. Many committees pick the venue and the honorees first and come to the entertainment later, and we're happy to hear from you at any point. Tell us the date and the venue, and we'll come back with a recommendation and a price.
